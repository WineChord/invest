# Decision Retrospective Template

Use this after a material monthly decision, full-cycle run, discovery sprint, or community-led triage has enough elapsed evidence to judge process quality. This is not a performance boast and not a public trading signal.

```yaml
review_id:
as_of:
lookback_start:
lookback_end:
decision_or_scan_path:
policy_version:
retrieved_at:
```

## Questions

- Which candidates were rejected, ignored, or left in low priority and later became material enough to revisit?
- Which community leads were not sent to a primary-source skim, and was that still justified?
- Which price-discipline decisions were too conservative, too aggressive, or still correct?
- Which active candidates were protected by the process from avoidable ruin, dilution, stale evidence, or community hype?
- Which source, scoring, lane, macro, financing, or dashboard gap should be fixed durably?
