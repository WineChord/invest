# Decision Retrospective Template

Use this after a material monthly decision, full-cycle run, discovery sprint, or community-led triage has enough elapsed evidence to judge process quality. This is not a performance boast and not a public trading signal.

```yaml
review_id:

lookback_end:

policy_version:
retrieved_at:
```

## Questions

- Which candidates were rejected, ignored, or left in low priority and later became material enough to revisit?
- Which community leads were not sent to a primary-source skim, and was that still justified?
- Which price-discipline decisions were too conservative, too aggressive, or still correct?
- Using only information available at the time, how did zero exposure compare with the smallest mission-consistent staged exposure and the actual decision?
- What was the strongest rejected opportunity, what evidence was available then, when did the next evidence become available, and what was the 30/60/90-day price and fundamental path?
- Did the process create a Type-I error (bad inclusion) or Type-II error (missed opportunity), and which early signal could have revealed it?
- Did a starter remain immaterial after scale milestones were met, did new contributions dilute a winner's portfolio impact, or did a symbolic trade mask persistent high liquidity-option weight?
- What cash opportunity cost was accepted, and was the stated next evidence deadline actually honored?
- Which active candidates were protected by the process from avoidable ruin, dilution, stale evidence, or community hype?
- Which source, scoring, lane, macro, financing, or dashboard gap should be fixed durably?
