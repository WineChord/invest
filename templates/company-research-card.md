## Key Metrics to Track

Consider the metrics that actually connect to the thesis:

- addressable profit pool and why this company can capture it;
- bottleneck ownership, network effects, scarcity, licenses, technical edge, manufacturing capacity, or distribution leverage;
- revenue growth and growth durability;
- gross margin and operating leverage;
- free cash flow and cash conversion;
- cash, debt, burn rate, and runway;
- share count, stock-based compensation, warrants, convertibles, and other dilution paths;
- customer concentration and dependency risk;
- backlog, RPO, bookings, orders, launch cadence, production capacity, or equivalent operating metrics when applicable;
- guidance changes and management credibility;
- governance, internal controls, auditor quality, and accounting risk;
- competitive intensity, substitute risk, retention, pricing power, and product-market fit;
- regulatory, technical, and execution milestones;
- current market capitalization, enterprise value, and valuation state.
