# QSI Form 10-Q Readiness Review — 2026-07-23

Source: Quantum-Si Form 10-Q for the quarter ended 2026-03-31, published 2026-05-07 and retrieved 2026-07-23.

The filing establishes a real single-molecule protein-sequencing business and a planned year-end Proteus launch, but it does not establish current product-market fit. First-quarter revenue fell 69.4% to USD 0.258 million and gross profit fell to USD 0.074 million. Net loss was USD 21.7 million and operating cash use was USD 25.6 million.

Liquidity is the strongest positive. Cash, cash equivalents and current and non-current marketable securities totaled USD 190.4 million. At the July 22 completed close of USD 0.80 and 217.8 million May 4 basic shares, basic equity value is approximately USD 174 million. That comparison is not a liquidation value: future burn, liabilities, inventory, execution costs and 44.4 million excluded options, RSUs and warrants materially reduce the apparent cushion.

Proteus aims to increase throughput, automate workflows and provide amino-acid-level single-molecule measurements. Management says customers delayed legacy purchases ahead of launch, but current evidence does not prove that deferred demand will become paid placements, recurring consumables revenue or positive gross margin.

Conclusion: incubate QSI at C+ research-only, buy zero, with no executable limit or target. Require an on-time Proteus launch, independent customer validation, paid instrument placements, recurring consumables pull-through, improving gross margin, controlled cash use and reconciled dilution before any promotion review.
