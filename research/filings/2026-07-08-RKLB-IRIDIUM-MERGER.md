## Facts Reviewed

Rocket Lab entered into a definitive merger agreement with Iridium on 2026-06-28 and filed the event on 2026-06-29. The transaction would acquire Iridium for a notional USD 54.00 per Iridium share in cash and Rocket Lab stock, implying approximately USD 8.0 billion of Iridium enterprise value. The stock portion uses a collar tied to Rocket Lab's 10-day VWAP, and Rocket Lab disclosed committed bridge financing support for the cash portion.

Iridium brings an operating LEO communications network, L-band spectrum, subscriber base, recurring service revenue, and defense/emergency communications relevance. For Rocket Lab, this is a major shift from launch plus space systems toward a vertically integrated owner/operator of communications infrastructure.

## Mission Impact

The deal strengthens the multi-decade upside thesis if Rocket Lab can integrate Iridium without damaging balance-sheet survival. It adds an installed network and spectrum layer that Rocket Lab could not quickly build from scratch, and it may reduce the weakness of being mainly a launch and spacecraft supplier.

The acquisition also changes the risk profile. Rocket Lab becomes exposed to large-deal integration, communications regulation, shareholder and regulatory approvals, bridge financing, refinancing risk, and stock dilution. A stronger strategic asset does not automatically justify a larger order because the company is taking on a materially more complex capital structure and execution path.
