## Executive Conclusion

The filing weakens entry quality and confirms dilution risk. Oklo entered a new at-the-market equity program for up to USD 1.0 billion of Class A common stock. The filing also says the prior ATM program allowed up to USD 1.5 billion and that Oklo sold 15,774,224 shares for about USD 1.4999 billion before terminating that prior program.

## What Must Be Read

The material sections are the sales agreement summary, prospectus supplement, prior ATM usage disclosure, use of proceeds, risk factors, and plan of distribution.

## Thesis Delta

The advanced nuclear thesis remains intact but pre-commercial. The filing reinforces that Oklo can raise capital while its stock is strong, but it also makes future ownership dilution central to any promotion review.

## Valuation Delta

Entry remains too uncertain. A large ATM program at a pre-commercial company makes valuation harder to underwrite even if the power-scarcity lane is attractive.

## Risk Delta

Dilution risk increased. The funding option may support development, but the shareholder return path depends on licensing, deployment, fuel access, customer contracts, and capital discipline.
