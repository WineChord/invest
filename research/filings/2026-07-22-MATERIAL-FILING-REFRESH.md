Classification: `high`, reviewed; thesis `strengthened`, entry `unchanged_or_still_blocked`, research-only `B-`, buy 0.

Construction is substantially complete at the new Tennessee and Idaho TRISO facilities, and the U.S. Department of Energy approved Preliminary Documented Safety Analyses for both. The issuer says the two facilities could initially add up to 1 metric ton of uranium of annual capacity each and up to 5 metric tons collectively after full scale-up; its existing SN-0 line can produce up to 0.5 metric tons annually. Start-up, commissioning, licensing, qualification, and final readiness reviews remain incomplete, and full DOE authorization has not been received. The milestone strengthens manufacturing-execution evidence but does not establish recognized revenue, funded-backlog conversion, repeat delivery, gross margin, customer concentration, feedstock availability, or per-share cash generation. The USD 8.91 July 21 completed close predates this event; the filing-supported 160,673,542 base shares imply approximately USD 1.43 billion of pre-event basic equity value before options and unused plan capacity, and no post-event completed close is yet available. The entry and promotion gates remain closed.

Source-quality note: the 8-K cover, SEC acceptance timestamp, and attached press release are dated July 22, while Item 8.01 says the press release was issued July 17. This review uses `source_published_at=2026-07-22` and does not infer a July 17 milestone date.

## QTREX Quantum

Primary sources: [Form 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1837493/000118518526003063/qtrex6k072026.htm), [Form S-8](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1837493/000118518526003062/qtrexs8072026.htm), and [Form F-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1837493/000118518526003070/qtrexf1072126.htm), published and accepted 2026-07-21; retrieved 2026-07-22.

Classification: `critical`, reviewed; thesis `weakened`, entry `less_attractive`, research-only `C+`, buy 0.

The board increased the Amended and Restated 2019 Equity Incentive Plan reserve by 3,834,244 shares to 15,710,221, and the S-8 registered the incremental shares. The preliminary F-1 seeks to register the resale of up to 6,785,715 shares underlying USD 0.70 warrants; no later EFFECT filing existed at the audit cutoff, so the registration is not treated as effective, exercised, sold, or completed. Cash exercise could provide the issuer approximately USD 4.75 million, while any resale proceeds would go to the selling holder. The filing reports 55,708,771 ordinary shares outstanding as of July 19, plus 522,897 options, 8,637,605 unvested RSUs—1,100,000 subject to shareholder approval—the proposed resale warrant shares, and other warrants. It says existing cash is expected to fund only about six months of operations, substantial additional capital is needed for the next twelve months, and substantial doubt about going concern remains. The newly reconciled basic share count raises July 21 basic market value at USD 1.18 to approximately USD 65.74 million and makes the already material equity overhang more explicit. All three filings were accepted after the July 21 regular-session close, so USD 1.18 is a pre-event completed close and no post-event completed close is available. Direct cryogenic-interconnect exposure remains worth monitoring, but qualification, repeat production revenue, positive gross margin, liquidity, governance, and diluted per-share economics do not support promotion or purchase.
