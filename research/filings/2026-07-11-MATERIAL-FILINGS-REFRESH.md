- **RKLB — high, hold/no add.** Peter Beck's Equatorial Trust sold 3,275,779 shares on 2026-07-06 through 2026-07-08 for about USD 286.4 million at about USD 87.43 weighted average. The 10b5-1 plan was adopted 2026-03-27, before the Iridium announcement, so the sale is not evidence of a post-deal escape. Its size is nevertheless a serious ownership-overhang signal. Together with a 33.95% account weight and Iridium financing/integration risk, it blocks new buying but does not establish a broken operating thesis. Source: [Form 4](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819994/000200101126000109/edgardoc.xml), filed 2026-07-08.
- **LEU — promotion evidence, no current entry.** The USD 900 million DOE contract materially strengthens strategic relevance, while firm fixed-price milestones, discretionary options, full terms deferred to the next 10-Q, TENEX supply, expansion capital, debt, and shelf capacity prevent headline-based buying. Sources: [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065059/000162828026046801/leu-20260630.htm), published 2026-07-02, and [Q1 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065059/000162828026030891/leu-20260331.htm), published 2026-05-06.
