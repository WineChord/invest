### First-Principles Analysis — NBIS

```yaml
first_principles_analysis:
  question_rebuilt_from_basics: Can the new capital finance AI-cloud capacity whose lifetime common-share cash return exceeds interest, principal accretion, GPU replacement capital, customer concentration, and dilution from the current denominator?
  irreducible_facts: Nebius priced USD 5.0 billion of new original principal, expects USD 4.94 billion of net proceeds before options, and expects to exchange USD 800 million of existing original principal for approximately 15.8 million shares; first-half capital expenditure was USD 8.1303 billion and operating cash flow was driven principally by a USD 4.395 billion increase in deferred revenue.
  binding_constraints: Project-level returns, GPU depreciation and replacement, utilization, customer concentration, prepaid delivery obligations, note accretion, existing and new conversion rights, and continued external financing determine common-share value capture.
  causal_chain: New debt can fund more data-center and GPU capacity and extend the construction runway; that capacity must be delivered, utilized, and monetized at durable margins; cash returns must then exceed interest, accretion, refresh capital, and share issuance before value per diluted share rises.
  inherited_assumptions_challenged: A 9.87 percent decline does not make a roughly USD 65.6 billion economic-basic denominator cheap, cash proceeds are not operating free cash flow, and the approximately 15.8 million exchange shares mostly accelerate already deep-in-the-money conversion rights rather than representing wholly incremental fully diluted shares.
  value_capture_or_mission_link: Full-stack AI cloud remains a direct compute bottleneck, but an approximately USD 83.8 billion illustrative diluted value after old and new conversion rights requires an approximately USD 838 billion outcome merely to reach ten times before further capital needs.
  disconfirming_evidence: First-half capital expenditure exceeded operating cash flow, most operating cash flow came from customer prepayments, three large customers represented 59 percent of second-quarter revenue, and funded capacity has not yet produced disclosed lifetime GAAP cash returns per diluted share.
  decision_consequence: Retain B+ watch, no promotion, no buy zone, no top-five entry, purchase quantity zero, no executable limit, and rerun on settlement and final share and option evidence.
```

The pricing release supersedes the initial proposal where the terms differ. The base issue is USD 3.0 billion of 0.50% notes due 2030 and USD 2.0 billion of 4.50% notes due 2034, with options for another USD 450 million and USD 300 million. The initial conversion prices are approximately USD 313.46 and USD 324.65, premiums of 40% and 45% to the August 19 close. Accreted principal reaches 110% and 125% of original principal at maturity, producing stated effective maturity conversion prices of approximately USD 344.81 and USD 405.82. Base annual cash interest is approximately USD 105 million, while base maturity principal accretes to approximately USD 5.8 billion if not earlier repurchased, redeemed, or converted.

The company expects to exchange USD 400 million of each existing 2029 and 2031 note series for approximately 15.8 million Class A shares and expects both the new issuance and exchange to settle around August 24, subject to customary conditions. The exchange can reduce future debt service, but the shares may create near-term sale or hedge-unwind pressure. Existing conversion rights must be removed before measuring incremental dilution; the full 15.8 million shares cannot be counted once as exchange shares and again as conversion exposure.

The June 30 primary statements show USD 8.0421 billion of cash, USD 8.4990 billion of convertible-note carrying amount, 271.855218 million ordinary shares, and 21.065936 million near-zero exercise-price pre-funded warrants. At USD 223.90, those two common-equity layers imply an economic-basic value of approximately USD 65.6 billion. The new base notes represent about 15.731 million initial conversion shares; including the older convertible-note rights and the new base issue produces an illustrative diluted value near USD 83.8 billion before options, employee awards, financing use, and any reconciliation adjustment. These are analytical sensitivities, not a fair-value estimate.

The financing materially strengthens liquidity and supports construction, while second-quarter AI-cloud revenue and adjusted EBITDA demonstrate real demand and utilization. It does not yet prove that the enormous reinvestment program produces attractive GAAP cash returns after GPU depreciation, financing costs, and dilution. A bounded starter could limit loss, but position size cannot repair the unproved project-return chain or the high starting denominator. Waiting for the expected settlement and exact option and exchange outcome has low near-term cash opportunity cost, so cash and the existing IREN starter remain superior.

The new release is a legitimate technical lead, but it does not establish a manufacturing or commercial component. At USD 1.12 and approximately 55.709 million basic shares, basic equity value is about USD 62.4 million. Listed options, RSUs, and warrants raise the known potential denominator to approximately 72.066 million shares, about 29.4% above basic, before deciding which instruments are vested or exercisable. The approximately 6.786 million USD 0.70 warrants could provide roughly USD 4.75 million if cash-exercised, but exercise and resale are unconfirmed. The one-day rise therefore makes entry less attractive without repairing evidence or survival.

Reopen only on quantitative cryogenic and high-frequency absorption, reproducibility and independent evidence, a named qualified customer with paid economics, and current cash, burn, capitalization, and patent or license ownership. The mission gate passes only for continued research; evidence, survival, entry, promotion, and opportunity-cost gates remain closed.
