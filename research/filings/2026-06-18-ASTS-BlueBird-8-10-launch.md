## Review

AST SpaceMobile announced that BlueBirds 8, 9, and 10 launched successfully from Cape Canaveral on a Falcon 9 at 2:39 a.m. EDT on 2026-06-17. The company says the satellites are next-generation BlueBirds designed for direct connectivity to standard smartphones, with BlueBirds 11, 12, and 13 in final preparation for shipment and BlueBirds through 37 in production.

This is a positive thesis delta. It reduces one immediate launch-timing risk and shows that ASTS can continue deploying the direct-to-device network after prior launch and schedule concerns. It does not complete the evidence gate by itself. The account still needs on-orbit deployment and operating evidence, network performance evidence, regulatory and MNO economics, financing discipline, and progress toward enough satellites for commercial service.

## Conclusion

The event supports keeping ASTS as a small convex direct-to-device sleeve and can justify only a tiny staged add if current quote and broker preview stay inside the refreshed private cap. It does not justify large sizing or a full-cash deployment.
