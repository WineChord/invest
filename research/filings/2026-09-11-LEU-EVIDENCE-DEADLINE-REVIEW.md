## Executive Conclusion

The September 9 filing strengthens the LEU thesis because it adds a commercial microreactor customer and customer prepayments to the domestic HALEU build-out. It does not disclose the quantities or economics needed to determine whether the contract creates positive dilution-adjusted value for common shareholders. The conclusion therefore remains `thesis strengthened, entry evidence still insufficient`; no account record or trade is changed.
