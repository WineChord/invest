## MDA acquisition financing

MDA Space priced CAD 600 million of 6.50% senior unsecured notes due 2033 at par. The company expects the notes to close on 2026-08-05 and plans to use the proceeds to fund the Blue Canyon Technologies acquisition and related costs. The notes are guaranteed by material subsidiaries and include a special mandatory-redemption mechanism if the acquisition does not close. They sit alongside CAD 250 million of existing 7.00% notes due 2030.

The financing improves transaction certainty but increases fixed financial obligations before the acquisition has proved integration, cash conversion, and return on invested capital. MDA remains a B+ active candidate, but current purchase quantity stays zero and there is no executable limit price.

Source: [MDA Space Form 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1857047/000110465926086388/tm2621200d1_6k.htm), source published 2026-07-24, retrieved 2026-07-25.

## QSI Nasdaq minimum-bid deficiency

Quantum-Si disclosed that Nasdaq notified the company on 2026-07-23 that its Class A common stock had closed below USD 1.00 for 30 consecutive business days. The notice has no immediate effect on the listing. The initial compliance period runs through 2027-01-19, subject to Nasdaq's rules and any available second compliance period.

The notice adds a concrete delisting or reverse-split risk to an already pre-scale, cash-consuming business. It weakens entry attractiveness and reinforces research-only status, a purchase quantity of zero, and no executable limit.

Source: [Quantum-Si Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1816431/000181643126000049/qsi-20260723.htm), source published 2026-07-24, retrieved 2026-07-25.

## QTEX equity authorization and governance proposals

QTREX Quantum's meeting materials seek shareholder approval for equity grants of up to 550,000 restricted share units for the chief executive, 550,000 for the chair, and 270,000 in aggregate for non-executive directors. They also seek to increase authorized shares from 100 million to 300 million. The proposals permit equity compensation to replace salary and include a mechanism for settling accrued unpaid salary at a discount of up to 50%, alongside governance amendments that may affect shareholder rights and takeover defenses.

The proposals do not prove commercial traction and materially increase potential dilution and governance uncertainty. QTEX remains research-only with a purchase quantity of zero and no executable limit.

Source: [QTREX Quantum Form 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1837493/000118518526003120/qtrex6k072326.htm), source published 2026-07-24, retrieved 2026-07-25.
