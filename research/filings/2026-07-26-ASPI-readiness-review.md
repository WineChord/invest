# ASPI Readiness Filing Review — 2026-07-26

## Scope

This review covers ASP Isotopes' May 20, 2026 Form 10-Q, July 15 note-exchange Form 8-K, July 16 resale Form S-3ASR, July 16 recast Form 8-K, July 21 UF6 research Form 8-K, the TerraPower agreement exhibits, and completed market data through July 24. It supports research-only discovery readiness and is not a promotion review.

## Financial and Operating Evidence

- The March 31 balance sheet reported USD 207.3 million of cash, USD 83.2 million of short-term investments, USD 58.3 million of debt, 125,903,447 shares outstanding, USD 17.8 million of quarterly operating cash use, and USD 6.1 million of capital spending.
- First-quarter continuing-operations revenue was about USD 4.2 million and came from radiopharmacy, LNG, and TerraPower collaboration activity. The filing reported no enriched-isotope product sales.
- Management described planned initial carbon-14 and ytterbium-176 shipments in the third quarter and silicon-28 during 2026. These remain forward-looking milestones rather than completed customer acceptance or recurring revenue.
- Material weaknesses in internal control remained unresolved. Historical results were recast after the Skyline deconsolidation, increasing the importance of using current continuing-operations data.

## HALEU and Customer Evidence

The TerraPower agreements establish strategically relevant customer interest: potential initial-core supply, a potential ten-year relationship for up to 150 metric tons, and conditional financing. They also require licensing, permits, technical performance, and other conditions and include termination rights. The July 21 Texas A&M Engineering Experiment Station agreement supports uranium-hexafluoride feedstock research, but it is not production approval, customer qualification, commercial delivery, or margin evidence.

## Capitalization and Dilution

On July 15, QLE agreed to exchange about USD 109.2 million of convertible-note principal plus accrued interest for 23,160,682 newly issued ASPI shares. The July 16 resale registration covers those shares, and ASPI receives no proceeds from their resale. Shares outstanding were 153,309,380 after the exchange, roughly 21.8 percent above the March 31 count. About USD 110.7 million of QLE note principal was expected to remain outstanding after the transaction.

## Decision Effect

The strategic bottleneck fit is real enough for C+ research-only coverage, but the evidence does not clear the entry gate. No commercial isotope or HALEU product revenue, unproved yield and margin, permits, conditional contracts, continued cash use, capital needs, remaining notes, resale supply, internal-control weaknesses, and a roughly USD 593 million indicated market capitalization all remain binding. The completed July 24 close of USD 3.87 was down 45.18 percent over 21 sessions and 52.28 percent over 126 sessions, but price decline does not substitute for qualified production or per-share economics. Quantity is zero and no executable limit exists.
