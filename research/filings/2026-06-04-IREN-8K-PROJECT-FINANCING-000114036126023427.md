## Thesis Delta

The power-backed AI cloud thesis is more executable because financing exists for the Microsoft-related GPU infrastructure. Equity risk also increased because the project relies on contract acceptance, debt-service coverage, GPU deployment economics, parent support, and power hedges.

## Valuation Delta

Entry remains too uncertain. IREN stays watch-only because financing complexity, bitcoin exposure, customer concentration, and leverage can overwhelm common-stock upside.

## Risk Delta

The filing adds direct financial obligations and limited parent guarantee exposure. It supports no-buy status until project-level unit economics, acceptance, utilization, and covenant headroom are clearer.
