## Material findings

### MDA

MDA reported second-quarter revenue of CAD 498.6 million, up 33.6% year over year, adjusted EBITDA of CAD 96.3 million, up 26.2%, and bookings of CAD 808.9 million. Backlog rose approximately CAD 310 million sequentially to CAD 4.003 billion, although it remained below CAD 4.568 billion a year earlier. Full-year guidance was narrowed to revenue of CAD 1.8 billion to CAD 1.9 billion and adjusted EBITDA of CAD 330 million to CAD 370 million.

The operating evidence strengthened, but per-share cash quality remains mixed. Second-quarter operating cash flow was negative CAD 93.4 million and free cash flow was negative CAD 150.2 million. Diluted weighted-average shares increased about 11.3% year over year before the full effect of the post-quarter 23 million-share financing. The June 30 cash and debt snapshot also predates the CAD 600 million notes and the pending Blue Canyon and CLS acquisitions, so it is not a pro-forma leverage measure. The confirmed six-share starter remains held; further scaling requires evidence of working-capital reversal, fully diluted capitalization, acquisition closing and integration, and combined cash conversion.

Source: [MDA second-quarter 2026 Form 6-K and Exhibit 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1857047/000110465926092384/tm2621766d2_ex99-1.htm), published 2026-08-07 and retrieved 2026-08-09.

### GSAT

Globalstar disclosed a pending Amazon acquisition under which holders may elect USD 90 in cash or Amazon shares, with aggregate cash elections capped at 40% and the consideration subject to a satellite-milestone adjustment currently estimated at approximately USD 97 million. Thermo, which controls approximately 57.6% of the voting power, has approved the transaction, while SEC, FCC, French and other closing conditions remain outstanding.

The transaction caps the independent-company upside and changes the remaining setup into merger-event risk. That no longer fits this account's multi-decade asymmetric mission. GSAT is frozen outside the new-buy opportunity ranking and retained only as satellite-network, Apple, Amazon and MDA customer intelligence until the transaction closes, fails or materially changes.

Source: [Globalstar second-quarter 2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1366868/000136686826000039/gsat-20260630.htm), published 2026-08-06 and retrieved 2026-08-09.

### OKLO

Oklo reported USD 1.21 million of quarterly revenue from acquired engineering services rather than Aurora power sales. First-half net loss was USD 81.6 million, while cash and marketable debt securities were approximately USD 3.006 billion after substantial ATM issuance. Shares outstanding increased about 15.3% from year-end, and the company expects 2026 operating cash use of USD 120 million to USD 150 million plus USD 400 million to USD 500 million of property and equipment spending.

DOE and NRC preliminary-document approvals strengthen regulatory progress and the large cash balance improves survival, but neither proves a licensed commercial reactor, binding power economics, fuel availability, unit cost or dilution-adjusted value capture. Status remains watch-only with no buy-zone promotion.

Source: [Oklo second-quarter 2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849056/000162828026054571/oklo-20260630.htm), published 2026-08-07 and retrieved 2026-08-09.

### SEI

Solaris Energy Infrastructure reported quarterly revenue of USD 219.4 million, net income of USD 20.5 million and first-half operating cash flow of USD 265.5 million. Its amended Hatchbo arrangement covers approximately 660 MW under a ten-year base term plus an eight-year extension and is expected to begin revenue recognition in 2027.

This is the strongest positive filing delta outside MDA, but the capital structure still blocks promotion. June cash was USD 824.1 million against approximately USD 1.609 billion of long-term debt and USD 882.7 million of convertible notes; the company also disclosed heavy equipment commitments and about USD 1 billion of remaining 2026 capital spending. SEI remains a B watch candidate pending customer-credit, project-return, financing, guarantee and fully diluted enterprise-value work.

Source: [Solaris Energy Infrastructure second-quarter 2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1697500/000162828026054349/sei-20260630.htm), published 2026-08-06 and retrieved 2026-08-09.

### AAOI

Applied Optoelectronics reported quarterly revenue of USD 191.9 million, up 86.4%, while gross margin declined to 27.7% and net loss widened to USD 22.8 million. First-half operating cash flow was negative USD 73.8 million and capital expenditure was USD 335.1 million. Cash growth relied heavily on approximately USD 1.03 billion of ATM proceeds; customer and warrant concentration remain material.

AI optical demand strengthened, but normalized free cash flow, utilization, yield, concentration and fully diluted share economics do not support promotion from C+ research-only coverage.

Source: [Applied Optoelectronics second-quarter 2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1158114/000143774926026278/aaoi20260630_10q.htm), published 2026-08-06 and retrieved 2026-08-09.

### QBTS, RDW and RGTI

D-Wave's quarterly revenue remained only USD 3.08 million and first-half operating loss was USD 108.0 million. The Quantum Circuits acquisition expanded technical breadth but consumed large cash and stock consideration and created substantial goodwill. Redwire's quarterly revenue and book-to-bill improved, but acquisition mix, contract-estimate adjustments, approximately 30% share growth since February, remaining ATM capacity and material weaknesses keep per-share quality unresolved. Rigetti's quarterly revenue reached USD 5.14 million from a small base and its 108-qubit system is available, but operating losses, internal rather than independent fidelity evidence and the gap between commercial scale and market value remain decisive.

All three remain C+ research-only comparators without promotion or buy-zone eligibility.

Sources: [D-Wave Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1907982/000190798226000129/qbts-20260630.htm), [Redwire Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819810/000181981026000124/rdw-20260630.htm), and [Rigetti Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1838359/000110465926091993/rgti-20260630x10q.htm), all published 2026-08-06 and retrieved 2026-08-09.

## Decision implication

MDA remains the strongest current operating candidate and SEI the most important filing-driven challenger. MDA's results strengthen the hold thesis but do not establish fully underwritten cash conversion or acquisition economics. GSAT leaves the new-buy ranking because the pending Amazon transaction caps the long-duration right tail. The remaining filings improve lane intelligence without creating a current promotion or buy-zone change.
