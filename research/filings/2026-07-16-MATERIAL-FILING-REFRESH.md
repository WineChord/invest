## AST SpaceMobile

Primary sources:

- [Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1780312/000149315226033365/form8-k.htm), published and accepted 2026-07-15; retrieved 2026-07-16.
- [Exhibit 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1780312/000149315226033365/ex99-1.htm), published 2026-07-15; retrieved 2026-07-16.
- [Pricing release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260715092237/en/AST-SpaceMobile-Announces-Pricing-of-Private-Offering-of-%241.0-Billion-of-Convertible-Senior-Notes-Due-2034-Effective-Conversion-Price-of-%24149.20-per-Share-with-Capped-Call), published 2026-07-15; retrieved 2026-07-16.

Classification: `high`, reviewed; thesis `weakened`, entry `too_uncertain`, no new buy.

AST SpaceMobile priced a USD 1.0 billion private offering of 1.625% senior unsecured convertible notes due February 1, 2034, with a 13-day option for up to another USD 150 million. Settlement is expected July 20, subject to customary conditions. The initial conversion price is approximately USD 79.57 per share, 20% above the issuer-reported USD 66.31 July 15 Nasdaq close; the initial capped-call cap is USD 149.20 per share. At the initial conversion rate, the base principal corresponds to approximately 12.57 million shares before adjustments and before the company's cash-or-share settlement election. The capped calls can reduce economic dilution above the initial conversion price only up to their cap; they do not eliminate principal repayment, hedge-flow, or execution risk.

Estimated net proceeds are about USD 983.6 million before approximately USD 96.9 million of capped-call cost. The remaining proceeds are intended for additional access to orbit, growth initiatives, partnerships, acquisitions, and vertical integration; the company says it currently has no agreement for those strategic transactions. The terms are more shareholder-friendly than an immediate common offering, but another large financing while the stock is below prior peaks and the use of proceeds remains broad raises capital-allocation and management-credibility risk.

The same filing gives preliminary unaudited June 30 cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash of about USD 2.723 billion, down from approximately USD 3.5 billion reported at March 31. The categories and quarter-end timing prevent treating the approximately USD 777 million difference as a clean operating-burn measure, but the decline reinforces the capital intensity of constellation deployment. Pro forma liquidity after the base offering may exceed USD 3.6 billion before capped-call cost and later spending, which improves survival capacity while increasing debt and capital-allocation exposure.

The launch campaign now targets approximately 45 BlueBird satellites in early 2027, subject to assembly, testing, launch-vehicle, logistics, and other dependencies. That timing does not invalidate the direct-to-device thesis, but it extends the path to scale and raises sensitivity to launch access and cash deployment. Advanced Rakuten discussions could support a Japanese project worth up to JPY 148 billion, about USD 1 billion, but neither a joint venture nor government financing is assured and neither can be counted as funded economics.

Decision consequence: retain the confirmed 11-share holding without adding or selling solely on the financing. The pricing removes the unknown-term blocker but does not clear the entry gate: settlement and the option remain open, the approximately 12.57 million-share initial conversion exposure and hedge mechanics are material, use of proceeds is broad, deployment and monetization are not yet proven, and the account already has a concentrated ASTS position. Reopen immediately upon settlement or cancellation, option exercise, a material launch or regulatory update, a specific strategic transaction, or a new completed regular-session close after the announcement.

## Gilat Satellite Networks

Primary source:

- [Form 6-K and press release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/897322/000117891326003492/zk2635686.htm), published and accepted 2026-07-15; retrieved 2026-07-16.

Classification: `medium`, reviewed; thesis `strengthened`, entry remains `trigger_only`, no new buy.

Gilat reported more than USD 20 million of SkyEdge orders from one leading global satellite operator for services, ground systems, and network management. Most deliveries are expected within 24 months. The award supports continued demand for Gilat's multi-orbit ground technology and improves backlog quality, but the customer is unnamed and the filing does not disclose margin, cash-conversion, cancellation, or concentration economics.

Decision consequence: the evidence strengthens the active-candidate thesis but does not clear the entry gate. The proposed Comtech transaction, combined leverage and integration, contract margin and cash conversion, customer concentration, and the account's existing space exposure remain more important than this order alone. Keep `trigger_only`; reopen on Comtech closing terms, combined financial evidence, a larger contract with economics, or a thesis-preserving valuation dislocation.

The ASTS financing and deployment update is the only new event capable of changing current holding or sizing judgment. GILT's order improves evidence without overcoming transaction and portfolio opportunity-cost constraints. No reachable material filing or pricing evidence remains unreviewed; ASTS settlement, option exercise, and later use of proceeds are genuinely external future events and must trigger fresh review rather than be estimated.
