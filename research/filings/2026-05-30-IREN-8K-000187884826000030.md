## Review Summary

IREN disclosed that a wholly owned subsidiary entered purchase documentation with Dell Marketing L.P. for GPUs and related products and services with an aggregate purchase price of approximately USD 1.6 billion. The filing says the GPUs will be deployed at IREN's Childress, Texas campus to service the previously announced USD 3.4 billion managed services AI cloud contract, and IREN agreed to unconditionally guarantee the subsidiary's obligations.

This is stronger source-backed evidence that IREN is trying to convert power access into AI cloud capacity, not only bitcoin mining optionality. It also increases the importance of financing discipline, shipment timing, customer economics, deployment execution, and parent-level guarantee risk. The purchase commitment is large relative to the current thesis uncertainty.

## Decision Use

Do not treat this review as a buy order. IREN remains a `watch` candidate with `too_uncertain` entry quality. The 8-K strengthens the AI cloud execution path but does not remove bitcoin exposure, financing risk, customer concentration risk, or uncertainty around durable unit economics.
