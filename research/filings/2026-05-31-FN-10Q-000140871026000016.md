## Review Summary

Fabrinet's fiscal Q3 2026 filing supports research-only watchlist coverage because it gives direct evidence of AI-relevant optical and datacom manufacturing exposure. Revenue for the quarter ended 2026-03-27 was USD 1.214 billion, up from USD 871.8 million in the prior-year quarter. GAAP net income was USD 125.2 million.

The filing is relevant to the semiconductor interconnect lane because Q3 optical communications revenue was about USD 888.7 million. Within that, datacom revenue was about USD 260.4 million and datacenter interconnect revenue was about USD 196.9 million. Non-optical communications revenue included about USD 106.7 million of high-performance computing revenue. This is enough direct exposure to monitor FN as an optical-manufacturing bottleneck peer.

The main promotion blockers are business-model and concentration risk. The company's gross margin remains low relative to IP-heavy interconnect names, and the filing's XBRL facts show four major customers represented 58.1% of revenue for the nine months ended 2026-03-27. That combination points to specialized manufacturing-service economics rather than clear owner-like pricing power.

## Decision Use

Add FN to the research universe as `research_only`, not `watch`, `active_candidate`, or a buy candidate. A future promotion would require evidence that datacom/HPC growth is durable, customer concentration is improving or economically favorable, and manufacturing capacity constraints are translating into shareholder-level pricing power rather than only higher volumes.
