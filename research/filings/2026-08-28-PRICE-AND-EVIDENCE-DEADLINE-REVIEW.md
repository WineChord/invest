## BRUN — Q2 filing resolves R1 evidence debt but not the entry gate

Boost Run provides bare-metal GPU servers and supporting power, network, security and deployment services. It therefore expresses the AI-compute bottleneck more directly than a weak thematic proxy. Three disclosed order sets total approximately USD 901.723 million, or 47.5 percent of management's USD 1.9 billion TCV claim: the May Thinking Machines orders are USD 471.7 million over 36 months; a June agreement is USD 207.565 million over three years with USD 18.972 million already recorded as deposits; and a July order is USD 222.458 million over four years with a non-refundable 25 percent prepayment schedule. The Thinking Machines order fees are non-cancelable and non-refundable except for termination after an uncured company breach, while the complete customer mix, credit support and full deployment economics remain undisclosed.

Second-quarter revenue was USD 31.138 million, up 270 percent year over year. The issuer's USD 29.421 million gross profit and 94.5 percent gross margin exclude USD 18.145 million of depreciation and amortization and USD 10.555 million of colocation lease cost, leaving only about USD 0.721 million before SG&A on a simple full-infrastructure-cost comparison. SG&A was USD 13.625 million and operating loss was USD 12.904 million. The quarter's USD 75.05 million net loss also included a USD 55.74 million tax provision tied to transaction accounting, so neither GAAP net loss nor headline gross margin alone describes steady-state economics.

Cash quality is the decisive evidence blocker. Six-month operating cash flow was USD 114.249 million, but customer deposits increased USD 112.949 million. The company also paid USD 99.933 million of finance-lease principal in financing cash flow. June 30 unrestricted cash was USD 120.166 million, while current liabilities were USD 178.698 million and the working-capital deficit was USD 23.58 million. Customer advances support near-term liquidity but are accompanied by future service obligations.

The obligation stack is large relative to current revenue. Present-value finance-lease liabilities were USD 238.126 million, operating-lease liabilities were USD 119.521 million and other financial obligations were USD 78.484 million. Two uncommenced colocation leases require USD 226.830 million of undiscounted payments. A five-year purchasing agreement has USD 1.440 billion of minimum commitments and a software arrangement has USD 100 million of consideration. These commitments can fund a much larger revenue base, but they also make utilization, customer credit and deployment timing survival-critical.

After the August 20 warrant redemption, the company reported 54,312,123 Class A shares plus 29,533,018 Class B shares, or 83,845,141 economic shares, and no warrants outstanding on August 25. At USD 20.61, current market capitalization is approximately USD 1.728 billion. Treating the full 9,214,301-share 2026 incentive-plan reserve as a conservative dilution envelope produces approximately 93.059 million shares and USD 1.918 billion of equity value, although an authorized reserve is not current issuance. The company says warrant exercises generated about USD 130 million of gross cash since the business combination, but current unrestricted cash after expansion spending is not reconciled; a current EV is therefore N/A rather than mixing the August share count with June cash. Current market value is about 13.9 times annualized Q2 revenue and 4.3 times management's unproved USD 400 million exit-ARR target. If the target were reached and the stated 15–20 percent future net-cash-flow margin were durable, current value would still represent roughly 21.6–28.8 times that target cash flow.

Disclosure controls were ineffective at June 30 because of IT-control, segregation-of-duties and accounting-personnel material weaknesses. The 10-Q does not reconcile the full USD 1.9 billion TCV by customer, deployment schedule, cancellation protection or margin. Those omissions are decision-critical for promotion, not a reason to discard BRUN from research.

### BRUN gate result

- Mission gate: conditional pass. Direct AI-compute capacity and a newly public denominator can support a material right tail if full-cost economics scale.
- Evidence gate: pass for R2, fail for promotion or purchase. One binding customer order and a filed quarter are enough for comparison, but total contract quality, concentration, cash conversion and controls remain unresolved.
- Entry gate: fail. The post-results close embeds substantial execution at about 13.9 times annualized Q2 revenue while obligations and incentive-plan dilution remain large.
- Survival gate: conditional. Cash and deposits cover near-term work, but current liabilities, finance leases and future commitments make deployment and customer credit critical.
- Mission-impact gate: uncertain. A small staged position could matter if the USD 1.9 billion TCV converts at durable margins, but the current evidence does not establish that causal chain or beat LEU, GILT, STDN, IREN and cash.

Keep BRUN `incubating / R2_comparable`, quantity zero, no promotion, no buy zone and no executable limit. Review contract concentration, customer credit, deployment milestones, cash flow after deposit changes and lease principal, control remediation, current cash after warrant proceeds and the incentive-plan award denominator by 2026-09-11 or sooner on a material filing.
