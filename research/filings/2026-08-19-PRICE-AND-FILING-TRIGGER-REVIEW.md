## Scope and Article 1 preflight

Fabrinet's fiscal 2026 Form 10-K and IREN's Mirantis Form D were the only new filings capable of changing a material company-level conclusion. The scope therefore covered those two filings, CRDO as the highest-priority price trigger, the remaining triggered issuers, confirmed holdings, the strongest same-lane and cross-lane opportunity-cost comparators, and the current macro regime.

The Article 1 sentinel passed before the review. This work advances Article 1 by testing whether a correlated semiconductor and optical selloff exposed a rare mission-consistent entry or whether new primary evidence changed dilution-adjusted shareholder value. It preserves the truth boundary by treating prices as research triggers, distinguishing non-cash acquisition consideration from issuer financing, and leaving broker-confirmed account records unchanged.

### First-Principles Analysis — FN

```yaml
first_principles_analysis:
  question_rebuilt_from_basics: Can Fabrinet's qualified optical-manufacturing capacity capture enough durable pricing and cash returns to create mission-scale diluted per-share upside from the current denominator?
  irreducible_facts: Fiscal 2026 revenue grew 35.7 percent to USD 4.641 billion; data-center and communications-infrastructure revenue grew about 40.7 percent and 47.2 percent; GAAP gross margin remained 12.0 percent; four customers supplied 57.4 percent of revenue; operating cash flow was USD 256.7 million against USD 252.5 million of cash property and equipment purchases.
  binding_constraints: Customer orders are short cycle and cancellable, qualification creates switching cost without transferring design IP, gross margin remains contract-manufacturing-like, working capital and capacity must be funded before returns are known, and the current approximately USD 17.3 billion basic value already requires sustained growth.
  causal_chain: AI optical demand can increase qualified manufacturing volume and utilization, but common shareholders receive exceptional value only if those volumes create durable margins, cash conversion and reinvestment returns after customer bargaining power and expansion capital.
  inherited_assumptions_challenged: Lower NVIDIA concentration does not mean lower aggregate concentration, revenue growth does not establish pricing power, and a 19 percent one-day decline does not create a mission-grade entry when the price returns near previously rejected levels.
  value_capture_or_mission_link: FN is a useful optical-capacity monitor, but a feasible position can become mission relevant only if specialized manufacturing captures more of the bottleneck rent rather than passing most economics to design and end customers.
  disconfirming_evidence: GAAP gross margin declined ten basis points year over year, four-customer concentration rose from 45.8 percent to 57.4 percent, inventory grew to USD 1.021 billion, simple operating cash flow less cash property and equipment purchases was only about USD 4.2 million, and fiscal 2027 capital expenditure is expected to rise.
  decision_consequence: Retain C+ research-only, no promotion, no buy-zone eligibility, no top-five entry and no portfolio action; reopen on durable pricing, customer commitments, expansion returns and cash conversion rather than price alone.
```

Fourth-quarter revenue was USD 1.316 billion, up 45% year over year, and fiscal 2026 GAAP net income was USD 473.0 million. First-quarter fiscal 2027 guidance of USD 1.375 billion to USD 1.425 billion implies approximately 6.4% sequential revenue growth at the midpoint. The demand and execution evidence therefore strengthened materially.

Shareholder capture did not strengthen at the same rate. GAAP gross margin was 12.0% for both the fourth quarter and full year, while Cisco, NVIDIA, Nokia and Amazon represented 19.9%, 16.3%, 10.7% and 10.5% of annual revenue. Operating cash flow fell from USD 328.4 million to USD 256.7 million as receivables and inventory absorbed cash; cash property and equipment purchases more than doubled to USD 252.5 million. Reported capital expenditure including accruals was USD 298.9 million, capital commitments were USD 108.1 million, and an August 17 approximately USD 75 million term loan supports further capital expenditure.

At USD 482.59 and 35.835 million basic shares, the basic equity value is approximately USD 17.29 billion, about 3.73 times fiscal 2026 sales and 36.6 times GAAP net income. FN survives comfortably and merits continued monitoring, but the filing improves the evidence gate without repairing the mission gate. The price is also close to the USD 474–487 range repeatedly reviewed in July without promotion. FN remains behind higher-value-capture optical candidates, the current leading candidates and cash in the opportunity-cost comparison.

### First-Principles Analysis — IREN

```yaml
first_principles_analysis:
  question_rebuilt_from_basics: Does the Form D show both what IREN paid for Mirantis and enough acquired cash flow to prove that the consideration creates diluted per-share value?
  irreducible_facts: The filing reports USD 464.866 million of shares issued to 183 recipients as upfront consideration for Mirantis shares; the 12.632234 million issued shares and a 13.673894 million aggregate share-equivalent consideration were already disclosed in the August 4 closing materials.
  binding_constraints: Form D offering value is a good-faith value for a non-cash private issuance, not issuer cash proceeds, an open-market sale, a target enterprise value or a current IREN valuation; Mirantis operating and integration economics remain absent.
  causal_chain: Mirantis must improve software capability, customer conversion, margins and project cash returns by more than the share-equivalent consideration, cash substitution, integration cost and later dilution before the acquisition increases value per IREN share.
  inherited_assumptions_challenged: All sold means the Rule 506 issuance was completed, not that recipients sold publicly; zero remaining means no shares remain in this filing, not that acquisition or future dilution risk is zero; USD 36.80 per issued share is not an entry price.
  value_capture_or_mission_link: A software and orchestration layer can improve IREN's full-stack AI-cloud value capture, but mission-relevant scaling requires GAAP revenue, margin and cash-return evidence rather than an administrative consideration value.
  disconfirming_evidence: The filing contains no Mirantis revenue, ARR, gross margin, operating cash flow, net debt, customer retention, integration cost, purchase-price allocation or project-level return evidence; Horizons 2-4 and GAAP AI-cloud cash returns also remain outstanding.
  decision_consequence: Retain B+ active-candidate and the confirmed five-share starter, add zero, preserve every scale blocker and make no promotion, top-five or account-action change.
```

The Form D amount divided by the 12,632,234 issued shares is exactly USD 36.80 per share, while the prior prospectus described total consideration as 13,673,894 share equivalents and cash substitution for unaccredited holders at USD 45.66. These are date- and filing-purpose-specific measurement bases, not competing estimates of IREN fair value. The new filing does not increase the already modeled immediate share count and does not show that IREN received cash.

Only the upfront issuance audit trail becomes more complete. Mirantis financials, purchase accounting, retention and integration costs, Horizons 2-4 acceptance, remaining capital expenditure and GAAP AI-cloud project returns remain decision-critical for any position above the bounded starter. Current status, rank and action therefore remain unchanged.

## Evidence reconciliation

Independent filing-quality, capitalization and counterargument checks reached the same boundary. FN's demand, execution and balance-sheet evidence strengthened, while the same filing strengthened the bear case on customer concentration, low gross margin and expansion cash returns. IREN's filing improved traceability but not acquired economics. No current price reset or macro combination changes an action, promotion, buy zone, top-five rank or Article 1 conclusion.

This is therefore a targeted evidence review, not a new allocation decision. The primary evidence resolves the question without changing the opportunity-set conclusion.

## Decision effect and Article 1 postflight

The new evidence improves the research record but not the portfolio decision. FN has stronger demand and execution than the prior review captured, yet its economic chain still ends in low-margin, concentrated and capital-consuming contract manufacturing before proving mission-grade value capture. IREN's new form records an already known share exchange without supplying Mirantis economics. The other triggers are either still expensive, ownership-only or unsupported by new operating evidence, and the macro overlay does not show a crisis.

No trade is executed or authorized, and no account fact changes. The result advances Article 1 by keeping a potentially important optical-capacity monitor and the existing IREN starter under evidence-based review while refusing to treat a sharp price move or administrative filing value as an allocation signal.
