## Thesis Delta

AST SpaceMobile remains one of the highest-upside public names in the watchlist because it could become a regulated direct-to-device connectivity layer. The Q1 filing and release support that path through FCC commercial authorization, the planned BlueBird 8-10 launch, BlueBird 11-33 production progress, and a larger cash base.

The thesis remains fragile. Revenue is still pre-scale, costs are rising, the company is making large satellite and spectrum investments, and common share count increased during the quarter. The investment case depends on launch cadence, satellite performance, MNO conversion, regulatory expansion, and eventual network economics.

## Valuation Delta

At the 2026-05-22 close of $105.86 and the 2026-05-26 pre-market indication around $113.07, the stock prices in significant success. The entry remains acceptable only as a small, option-like staged position. It is not appropriate for the full monthly deposit at an uncapped market price.

## Risk Delta

The main risks are launch delay, satellite manufacturing bottlenecks, technical underperformance, spectrum and regulatory constraints, MNO economics, capital intensity, debt, and future dilution. The proposed U.S. MNO joint venture is a positive ecosystem signal, but its final economics for AST SpaceMobile are not yet proven.
