## Review Summary

Circle remains a programmable-money infrastructure candidate, but valuation and business-model sensitivity are central. The prior review incorrectly treated a non-contiguous set of quarterly `other revenue` facts as total trailing-twelve-month revenue. The corrected TTM reconstruction is FY2025 USD 2.746642 billion, minus Q1 2025 USD 578.573 million, plus Q1 2026 USD 694.133 million, or **USD 2.862202 billion**. At the 2026-07-10 close of USD 66.14 and 248,576,030 Class A plus Class B shares outstanding on 2026-05-06, market capitalization is about USD 16.44 billion and gross price-to-sales is about **5.74**.

The companyfacts enterprise value was also wrong because the prior market-data selector preferred cash plus restricted cash over unrestricted cash on the same date. USD 76.894 billion of the reported total is segregated for stablecoin holders and is not corporate liquidity. At 2026-03-31, corporate cash was USD 1.517 billion and corporate-held stablecoin cash was USD 793 million. The automated metric now uses unrestricted cash; reserve assets remain excluded from enterprise-value cash.

TTM distribution, transaction, and other costs were USD 1.722785 billion, leaving revenue less distribution costs of about USD 1.139417 billion. Market capitalization is therefore about 14.4 times that more decision-useful measure. In Q1, reserve income supplied 94.0% of total revenue, while distribution and transaction costs consumed about 58.4% of total revenue. Coinbase alone received USD 330.6 million. Management's sensitivity table implies a 100-basis-point rate decrease would reduce annualized reserve income by about USD 773 million and distribution costs by about USD 384 million, a net headwind of roughly USD 389 million before other responses.

USDC circulation was USD 73.2 billion on 2026-07-09, about 5.0% below the 2026-03-31 balance. The first two ARC token presales total 807.5 million tokens and USD 242.25 million gross proceeds at USD 0.30, implying a USD 3.0 billion fully diluted network value. Token repayment conditions, future token ownership, and the split between token-holder and common-shareholder economics remain unresolved. Identified options, RSUs, contingent shares, plan capacity, and warrants create meaningful potential dilution, although they are not all immediately issuable.

Circle's 2026-07-10 final Office of the Comptroller of the Currency approval for Circle National Trust is a material regulatory positive. Initial capability is federally regulated digital-asset custody; future reserve management is planned, so the approval does not yet change the Q1 economics above.

## Decision Use

Do not treat this review as a buy order. Circle remains `watch` and receives zero current shares. The lower corrected gross sales multiple and stronger regulatory position are real positives, but the more relevant revenue-less-distribution-cost multiple, rate exposure, distributor leakage, circulation decline from quarter-end, dilution, and unclear ARC shareholder capture keep entry `too_uncertain`.
