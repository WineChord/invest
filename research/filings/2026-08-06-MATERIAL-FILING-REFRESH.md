Cash and investments were USD 3.0 billion at quarter-end and approximately USD 2.0 billion pro forma for SkyWater, so survival is not the immediate problem; valuation and shareholder-value capture are. The August 5 close of USD 39.93 predates the after-hours release. Status remains `watch`; current quantity is zero.
