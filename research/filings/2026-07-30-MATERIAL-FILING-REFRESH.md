NWPX remains B- research-only, quantity zero, with no executable limit. It is a stronger same-lane comparator and deserves a promotion review only if industrial water reuse, data-center water demand, durable pricing, and reinvestment economics become quantitatively visible.

Next review: signed backlog conversion, industrial and data-center end-market mix, project pricing, municipal funding, normalized input costs, cash conversion, reinvestment runway, and valuation.
