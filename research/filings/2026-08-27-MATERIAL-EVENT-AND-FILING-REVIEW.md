## STDN — commercial proof improves, entry remains unproved

Standard Nuclear reported second-quarter revenue of USD 4.736 million, gross profit of USD 3.183 million, operating loss of USD 4.296 million and net loss of USD 3.418 million. First-half operating cash use was USD 10.938 million and capital spending was USD 17.419 million. June 30 cash was USD 102.185 million; the company says the July IPO produced approximately USD 137.7 million of net proceeds, implying about USD 239.9 million of pro-forma cash and no debt.

The company delivered 50 kilograms of uranium to Radiant in the second quarter and the remaining core balance after quarter-end, completing its first commercial reactor core. That is meaningful customer-acceptance and production evidence. It does not yet show repeat utilization, depreciation, fixed-cost absorption, multi-customer gross margin or cash conversion: Q2 gross margin was approximately 67.2 percent, while first-half gross margin remained negative.

The backlog bridge must preserve customer identity and enforceability:

| 2026-08-26 | USD 119.3M | USD 443.5M | USD 14.1M | USD 576.9M |

The 1 MTU firm agreement plus customer options for up to 7 MTU is with **Antares Nuclear**, not Radiant. It explains approximately USD 57.4 million of the funded increase and USD 287.0 million of the option increase; the USD 9.0 million decline in unfunded backlog completes the USD 335.4 million total bridge. Radiant's separate August definitive agreement formalized a May binding term sheet already included in June backlog and did not increase total backlog. This distinction supersedes the earlier research phrasing that attributed the August backlog step to Radiant.

Only USD 119.3 million, or 20.7 percent, of current backlog is funded. USD 443.5 million, or 76.9 percent, is customer-controlled options. The issuer warns that cancellation or scope adjustment often leaves it without a contractual right to the reflected revenue. Antares accounts for roughly 48 percent of current funded backlog and 65 percent of options by arithmetic, while one fuel agreement represented USD 3.1 million, or 65.5 percent, of Q2 revenue. Customer credit support, take-or-pay protection and delivery economics remain unavailable.

SN-TN and SN-ID are substantially complete, but final documented safety analysis and readiness work remain. Authorization in the fourth quarter of 2026 is a target rather than an approval. Customers generally procure HALEU, so DOE allocation, transport, customer reactor licensing and funding can still delay fuel delivery independently of STDN's factory progress.

The final prospectus reports 149,095,234 Class A shares plus 11,578,308 Class B shares after the base offering, or 160,673,542 basic shares. The earnings release's approximate 154.2 million post-IPO figure conflicts with that filed denominator. Existing low-strike options add roughly 12.42 million shares; plan and employee-purchase reserves create further possible dilution but are not current basic shares. At USD 13.70, basic equity value is approximately USD 2.201 billion and pro-forma enterprise value is approximately USD 1.961 billion. The basic-plus-existing-option pressure value is about USD 2.37 billion before option exercise cash. These are comparison and stress-test values, not targets.

The smallest recorded mission-consistent starter would be 17 shares, costing USD 232.90 or 2.1112 percent of the USD 11,031.73 research NAV at the stale pre-event price. Size can bound account loss, but cannot make a base valuation case attractive or reconcile whether current enterprise value already capitalizes option conversion and future industrial economics. A USD 5–7 re-underwriting band is research-only scenario work, not an executable limit.

### STDN gate result

- Mission gate: pass. A reactor-agnostic domestic TRISO supplier can control a genuine long-duration nuclear-fuel bottleneck.
- Evidence gate: fail for promotion or purchase. The release is unaudited, no quarterly filing reconciles contract assets, customer concentration or shares, and repeat industrial economics remain absent.
- Entry gate: fail. The only completed price predates the material release, basic EV is about 16.4 times funded backlog and about 103.5 times annualized Q2 revenue, and a 10x outcome requires exceptional rather than base execution.
- Survival gate: pass at the present evidence boundary. Pro-forma cash is substantial and debt is absent, although additional capacity and working capital remain unbounded.
- Mission-impact gate: uncertain. A 2.11 percent starter could matter in a 10x outcome, but the current evidence does not support a credible path from base case to that outcome.

The independent valuation reviewer favored promotion to `watch`, and the allocation reviewer ranked STDN first conditionally after a post-event price. Discovery, filing and bear-case reviewers favored retaining `research_only`. Primary evidence resolves the conflict: the R2 stage and top-three challenger rank preserve urgency without treating an unfiled quarter and pre-event price as a stable status upgrade. Keep `B- / research_only`; review SEC filings, the event-after close, authorization, repeat delivery and contract protection by 2026-09-04.
