Parent and specified subsidiary guarantees are senior unsecured obligations. Guarantees create structural priority over the parent's 4.75% 2030 and 0.25% 2031 convertible claims to the extent described in the indenture; the new notes also rank ahead of subordinated intercompany convertible notes. Common equity is residual. Revolving availability is subject to the agreement and borrowing conditions, including no default and pro forma financial covenant compliance; a commitment increase is not drawn cash.

The general debt-incurrence fixed-charge coverage test is at least 2.0 times, but permitted-debt baskets include credit facilities up to the greater of USD 850 million or 25% of net tangible assets. It would be incorrect to present the coverage test as a universal leverage limit. The indenture also restricts payments, investments, transfers, liens and affiliate dealings with exceptions. A qualified new hyperscaler investment provision can use a net leverage test of no more than 2.5 times and reasonable expected cash-flow or revenue benefits over the following 18 months; covenant capacity is therefore not equivalent to cash already earned.

A change-of-control repurchase is at 101% in the specified circumstances. Optional redemption before April 1, 2029 uses the contractual make-whole mechanism. The equity claw can redeem up to 40% at 107%, with at least 50% remaining and the specified 180-day window. Scheduled prices are 103.5% in 2029, 101.75% in 2030 and par in 2031. Relevant default terms include cross-debt payment or acceleration of at least USD 100 million, judgments above USD 100 million, a 30-day interest default period, and generally 60 days after notice for covenant breaches, with 180 days for reporting breaches. Holders of 30% can accelerate under the indenture's applicable conditions.
