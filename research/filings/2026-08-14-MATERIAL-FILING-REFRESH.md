## Scope

## Decision-changing findings

### IREN

Microsoft accepted Horizon 1, the first 50 MW deployment in the five-year USD 9.7 billion contract, and NVIDIA granted the deployment GB300 NVL72 Exemplar Cloud status. The new evidence therefore warrants immediate promotion and entry review, not automatic purchase.

Source: SEC Form 8-K and Exhibit 99.1, published 2026-08-13 and retrieved 2026-08-14. Valid through the next annual filing, a Horizon 2–4 acceptance or delay, a financing event, a contract change or a material completed-close move.
