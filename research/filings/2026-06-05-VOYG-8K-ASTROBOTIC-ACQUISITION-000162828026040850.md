## Executive Conclusion

Voyager agreed to acquire Astrobotic Technology. The transaction is strategically relevant to space infrastructure and lunar logistics exposure, but it does not make VOYG buy-ready. The filing adds execution, integration, dilution, and contingent-consideration uncertainty on top of existing losses, negative Q1 gross profit, debt, Starlab uncertainty, and governance risk.

## Thesis Delta

The acquisition could broaden Voyager's space infrastructure footprint if Astrobotic closes and integrates well. It does not solve the prior blockers around margins, debt, Starlab funding and timing, and public operating proof.

## Valuation Delta

No promotion. VOYG remains research-only and below current buy-zone candidates because the transaction adds uncertainty before demonstrating shareholder-value capture.

## Risk Delta

Integration and dilution risk increased. Future reviews should check whether the acquisition closes in the expected second half of 2026, the final share issuance, earnout terms, cash costs, and any effect on backlog, margins, and cash burn.
