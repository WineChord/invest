## Thesis Delta

Rocket Lab is evolving from a small-launch company into a broader launch and space-systems platform. The filing supports the thesis through 63% revenue growth, 38.2% gross margin, lower operating loss as a percentage of revenue, and a larger backlog. The strongest positive update is that both launch services and space systems contributed to growth, which reduces dependence on any single product line.

The thesis is not de-risked. Neutron remains under development, operating expenses are still rising, and recent acquisitions increase integration risk. The securities litigation dismissal with prejudice reduces one governance/legal overhang, although derivative actions remain stayed pending final resolution.

## Valuation Delta

At the 2026-05-22 close of $135.76 and the 2026-05-26 pre-market indication around $143.00, the stock is no longer a clean value entry. The company has unusually strong execution evidence for the public space universe, but market capitalization already prices in a substantial part of the medium-term success case. Entry attractiveness is fair to expensive, not dislocated.

## Risk Delta

The key risks are Neutron schedule and technical execution, acquisition integration, dilution, and valuation compression after a sharp price run. The filing reports no material changes to risk factors from the 2025 10-K, but the operational and valuation setup makes position sizing important. A first purchase should be staged and price-limited rather than treated as a full allocation.
