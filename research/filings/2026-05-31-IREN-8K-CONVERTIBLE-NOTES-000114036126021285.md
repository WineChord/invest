## Executive Conclusion

The filing weakens IREN's entry quality without breaking the thesis. IREN issued USD 3.0 billion principal amount of 1.00% convertible senior notes due 2033, including the initial purchasers' full USD 400 million option exercise. Net proceeds were about USD 2.96 billion, and USD 201.3 million was used for capped calls. This improves liquidity for AI cloud buildout but adds leverage, future conversion dilution, and financing complexity.

## What Must Be Read

The material sections are the indenture and convertible-note summary, net-proceeds use, capped-call disclosure, maturity and interest terms, conversion mechanics, redemption conditions, and senior unsecured ranking.

## Thesis Delta

IREN's power-to-AI-cloud thesis remains plausible, especially after the separate Dell GPU purchase agreement. The convertible-note financing makes the thesis more executable but also more fragile: equity holders now depend on disciplined capital deployment, high utilization, customer economics, and the ability to absorb a much larger financing structure.

## Valuation Delta

Entry remains too uncertain. The notes do not make IREN buyable for the first USD 888 allocation because the stock still combines bitcoin exposure, AI customer uncertainty, GPU procurement risk, and now a large convertible-note stack.

## Risk Delta

Dilution and financing risk increased. The initial conversion price is about USD 73.07 per ordinary share, above the 2026-05-29 close, but future share settlement, capped-call limitations, and debt maturity risk matter if the AI cloud economics do not develop as planned.
