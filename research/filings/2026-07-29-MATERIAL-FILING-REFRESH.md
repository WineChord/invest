### Executive conclusion

The operating evidence strengthened materially, so the old mechanical trigger to wait for Q2 is satisfied. Bloom reported Q2 revenue of USD 1.065 billion, up 165.5% year over year, GAAP gross margin of 33.4%, operating income of USD 182.2 million, and Q2 operating cash flow of USD 226.4 million. It raised 2026 guidance to USD 3.9–4.2 billion of revenue, approximately 34% non-GAAP gross margin, USD 800–900 million of non-GAAP operating income, and USD 2.55–2.85 of non-GAAP EPS. Unrestricted cash was USD 2.667 billion at June 30.

The evidence gate nevertheless does not pass. The same 10-Q says the second of two Q2 customers representing 44% and 21% of revenue is a related party, which would imply roughly USD 224 million of Q2 revenue, but the related-party note reports only USD 2.818 million of Q2 related-party revenue. The filing does not reconcile the definitions or periods. The repository must not silently repair this apparent conflict for the issuer.

Revenue quality and per-share capture also remain constrained. One non-related customer represented 73% of first-half revenue; three customers represented 36%, 34%, and 17% of receivables. The filing says a reported customer may be a project-finance affiliate rather than the terminal user. Oracle's warrant arrangements produced 1.905 million issued shares plus 248,798 inducement shares and a customer-consideration asset whose amortization reduces revenue. Basic shares rose 4.75% from December 31 to June 30. Q2 gross profit also included USD 37.4 million of tariff recovery, while warranty and performance-guarantee exposures remain material. Scandium sourcing is not quantitatively reconciled.

At USD 167.035, the July 28 close is a pre-event price. Bloom requires a fresh post-event completed close and a fully diluted valuation refresh. The thesis is strengthened, but the entry state remains too uncertain and the status remains frozen pending a dedicated promotion review. Buy quantity is zero and no executable limit exists.

Next review: issuer reconciliation of the Q2 related-party contradiction; terminal-user and project-finance-affiliate economics; repeat cash conversion without deposit timing; Oracle consideration amortization; warranty and performance-guarantee exposure; quantitative scandium disclosure; and the first reliable post-event completed close.
