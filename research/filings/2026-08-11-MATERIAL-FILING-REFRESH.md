## Material findings

### RKLB

Rocket Lab reported second-quarter revenue of USD 234.1 million, up 62%, gross profit of USD 84.6 million and backlog of USD 2.356 billion, up 137%; management expects roughly 45% of backlog to convert in the next twelve months. The evidence strengthens the hold thesis, but first-half operating cash flow was negative USD 134.4 million, ordinary shares increased to 598.2 million before 41.0 million preferred shares, and the company raised approximately USD 1.52 billion through financing, primarily ATMs. Neutron Stage 1 tank production is aligned with a target pad delivery in Q4 2026; this is not a promised Q4 launch. The Iridium transaction still requires financing and approval. Hold 17 and add zero.

Source: [Rocket Lab second-quarter Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819994/000181999426000062/rklb-20260630.htm), published 2026-08-10 and retrieved 2026-08-11.

### ASTS

AST SpaceMobile reported USD 31.5 million of quarterly revenue, approximately USD 1.3 billion of backlog and thirteen satellites in orbit, with BlueBirds 14 through 16 ready and 17 through 46 in production. Commercial service revenue has not begun. First-half operating cash use was USD 145.2 million and property-and-equipment spending was USD 859.2 million, while post-quarter financing raised pro-forma liquidity above USD 3.7 billion and increased the capital-structure burden. Hold 11 and add zero pending service, carrier and fully diluted economics.

Source: [AST SpaceMobile second-quarter Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1780312/000119312526342550/asts-20260630.htm), published 2026-08-10 and retrieved 2026-08-11.

### RIOT

Riot signed a 191 MW, 20-year build-to-suit lease for a frontier-AI customer, with 96 MW targeted for December 2027 and 95 MW for June 2028. The stated USD 9.1 billion is nominal base rent over twenty years, not discounted backlog. Management estimates USD 2.1–2.3 billion of construction capital and USD 365–411 million of annual NOI after stabilization. A USD 573 million bridge priced at SOFR plus 2.75% matures on 2026-10-15; permanent financing and investment-grade credit support are not yet binding public facts. H1 operating cash flow was negative USD 272.8 million, Bitcoin sales funded liquidity, and ATM and convertible dilution remain material. The lease and completed AMD capacity clear R2/watch promotion, not active-candidate or buy-zone gates.

Source: [Riot second-quarter Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1167419/000110465926093448/riot-20260630x10q.htm), published 2026-08-10 and retrieved 2026-08-11.

### CRWV, GILT, IONQ and QUBT

CoreWeave added a USD 2.6 billion delayed-draw facility at SOFR plus 5.50%, secured and parent-guaranteed; direct AI demand remains strong, but expensive financing weakens common-equity downside. Gilat's first-half operating cash flow was negative USD 14.1 million, ordinary shares reached about 77.0 million, and the September 8 authorization vote could increase authorized shares from 90 million to 150 million. IonQ reported USD 80.1 million of quarterly revenue but low 24.9% gross margin, USD 120.3 million adjusted EBITDA loss, USD 270.4 million of H1 stock compensation, heavy cash use and large SkyWater cash-and-share consideration. Quantum Computing reported USD 5.55 million of acquisition-led revenue, negative gross profit, material cash use and unresolved controls. None clears promotion or entry.

Sources: [CoreWeave Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000357/crwv-20260807.htm), [Gilat Form 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/897322/000117891326003999/zk2635907.htm), [IonQ Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1824920/000119312526341001/ionq-20260630.htm), and [Quantum Computing Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758009/000121390026087267/ea0301433-8k_quantum.htm), all published 2026-08-10 and retrieved 2026-08-11.
