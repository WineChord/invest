## Review

The 2026-06-15 post-IPO 8-K confirms that SPCX is now a public common-stock security, but public tradability is not buy eligibility. The listing gives the account a direct public SpaceX expression, yet the company remains newly public, very large, governance-heavy, and still in early public price discovery.

The 2026-06-17 director appointment is governance context rather than a thesis-changing operating event. It does not resolve float, lockup, controlled-company, xAI/X, or shareholder-economics questions.

The 2026-06-22 Form 8-K and exhibit announced an inaugural offering of senior unsecured notes. The filing disclosed that SpaceX had approximately USD 100.8 billion of cash and cash equivalents as of 2026-06-19 and intended to use the net proceeds to repay outstanding bridge-loan borrowings in full, pay related fees and expenses, and use any remaining amount for general corporate purposes. That strengthens the capital-resources picture, but it also keeps capital structure and bridge-loan refinancing central to the public common-stock entry question.

## Conclusion

SPCX remains strategically important but not buy-ready. The post-IPO and notes disclosures strengthen the coverage case but do not clear the entry gate. A dedicated promotion review is required before SPCX can enter active candidate, buy-zone, or allocation consideration.
