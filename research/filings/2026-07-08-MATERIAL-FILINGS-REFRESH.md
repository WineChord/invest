## Executive Conclusion

The current SEC event-filing sweep found material events for seven watchlist names beyond the separate Rocket Lab Iridium merger review. The filings are current enough for the 2026-07-08 decision cycle, but none of them creates a new buy recommendation. LEU is the only non-holding whose thesis materially improves enough to warrant a future promotion review; SPCX, MU, IREN, CRWV, CRCL, and CBRS remain blocked by valuation, scale, capital structure, governance, short public history, or token-economics uncertainty.

## SPCX Notes Closing

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026045763/spcx-closing8xkjune2026.htm

SpaceX filed the closing terms for USD 25.0 billion aggregate principal amount of senior unsecured notes across 2031, 2033, 2036, 2046, and 2056 maturities. This strengthens the capital-resources and post-IPO financing record, but it also keeps leverage, governance, float, xAI/X exposure, and public common-stock entry complexity central. SPCX remains watch-only and cannot enter buy-zone consideration without a dedicated promotion review.

## MU Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000015/mu-20260528.htm

Micron's Q3 FY2026 filing confirms very strong current memory-cycle economics and AI/HBM relevance. Revenue was USD 41.456 billion for the quarter and gross margin reached 85%, driven by DRAM and NAND margin expansion and higher average selling prices. The same filing highlights HBM demand and supply-complexity risks. This keeps MU important for the semiconductor memory lane, but trillion-dollar scale and cyclical ASP risk block a current satellite-account buy.

## IREN Co-CEO RSU Grants

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000114036126027202/ef20077190_8k.htm

IREN disclosed grants of 9,099,328 restricted stock units to each co-CEO, subject to a combined six-year vesting and holding period. The holding period is a partial alignment feature, but the grant size adds governance and dilution pressure to a name already sensitive to financing, bitcoin exposure, AI cloud utilization, and customer economics. IREN remains watch-only.

## CRWV Senior Notes

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000291/crwv-20260618.htm

CoreWeave completed USD 1.25 billion of 9.625% senior notes due 2032 and EUR 2.0 billion of 8.500% senior notes due 2032. The financing supports the AI cloud buildout but materially reinforces leverage, refinancing, utilization, and GPU depreciation risk. CRWV remains watch-only and does not displace RKLB or ASTS for marginal capital.

## CRCL ARC Token Second Closing

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1876042/000187604226000205/crcl-20260629.htm

Circle sold 67.5 million additional ARC tokens at USD 0.30 per token for approximately USD 20.25 million of gross proceeds, with lockup and repayment conditions tied to the Arc network transition. The event adds programmable-money optionality but also increases token-economics complexity and does not resolve shareholder capture, reserve-income sensitivity, or regulatory risk. CRCL remains watch-only.

## CBRS Q1 2026 Form 10-Q

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2021728/000162828026044981/cbrs-20260331.htm

Cerebras' first post-IPO quarterly filing confirms real AI systems exposure and a public operating record, but customer concentration remains severe. Customer A accounted for 63% of Q1 2026 revenue and Customer B accounted for 11%; both are related parties under the filing's disclosure. CBRS should remain watch-only until at least two public quarters show durable customer diversification, economics, and post-listing execution.

## LEU DOE HALEU Contract

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065059/000162828026046801/leu-20260630.htm

Centrus disclosed a DOE contract to establish domestic commercial HALEU enrichment capacity, with USD 900 million of firm fixed-price milestone payments and options for additional HALEU deliveries. This is materially thesis-strengthening for the nuclear fuel bottleneck lane and makes LEU a better promotion-review candidate. It still does not create immediate buy eligibility because policy, contract execution, scale-up, funding, and valuation risks remain unresolved.
