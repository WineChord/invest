### Executive conclusion

The issuer release strengthens Rocket Lab's launch-cadence, defense-customer, and responsive-space evidence but does not clear the current entry gate.

Rocket Lab described a USD 266 million U.S. Space Force Rocket Systems Launch Program contract and separately stated that the program covers 12 HASTE launches with opportunities for up to six additional launches. The release does not clarify whether the stated USD 266 million includes the additional-launch opportunities. The first mission is planned no earlier than the end of 2026, and the work introduces a new launch location in Kodiak, Alaska. The award strengthens backlog-quality evidence and demonstrates that HASTE can support a multi-launch national-security program rather than only isolated demonstrations.

The release does not disclose program margin, termination economics, cash timing, capital spending, or the economics and contractual status of the six additional opportunities. It also does not resolve Neutron execution, the Iridium acquisition's approvals and financing, the USD 3 billion ATM capacity, integration risk, or the current valuation. At the July 27 completed close of USD 66.91 and the repository's current capitalization estimate, the equity value remains roughly USD 38.7 billion, far above a revenue-based margin-of-safety level.

### Executive conclusion

The filing preserves a material dilution and governance overhang without adding customer, utilization, margin, or cash-conversion evidence. Similar authority was approved at prior annual meetings, so the proposal is a recurring renewal rather than a newly created financing power.

The August 25 annual meeting materials ask shareholders to authorize the board for five years to issue additional shares up to 20% of issued share capital and to restrict or exclude pre-emption rights. They separately seek authority to repurchase up to 20% of issued share capital for 18 months and propose cancellation of 2,243,621 Class C shares. The repurchase and cancellation provisions may offset some dilution in specific circumstances, but the issuer did not commit to a net share reduction or limit the issuance authority to a particular funded project.

Renewal would preserve financing flexibility, but the combination of five-year duration, up to 20% issuance capacity, and excluded pre-emption rights keeps per-share dilution and governance risk material. It compounds existing secured leverage, pre-funded warrant, customer-concentration, capex, utilization, acquisition-integration, and cash-conversion uncertainty. Because similar authority already existed, the meeting notice does not by itself prove that those risks increased on July 27.
