- Retrieved for repository review: 2026-06-23.
- Source URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1807794/000162828026043303/crdo-20260502.htm

## Review

Credo remains one of the cleaner public expressions of the AI interconnect and connectivity bottleneck. The 10-K reinforces that the company sells into hyperscalers, neoclouds, OEMs, ODMs, and other networking ecosystem customers, and its product surface remains aligned with high-speed AI data-center connectivity.

The filing also keeps the main underwriting problem explicit. In fiscal 2026, Credo reported that sales to its top 10 customers were approximately 90% of total revenue, and that two customers each accounted for at least 10% of total revenue. That concentration can be rational in a hyperscaler-driven ramp, but it raises the bar for assuming durable pricing power, clean diversification, and stable growth beyond the current AI infrastructure cycle.

The refreshed repository market data for 2026-06-22 shows CRDO at USD 302.52, with a SEC-facts-derived market capitalization of about USD 56.4 billion and enterprise value of about USD 55.2 billion. That leaves too little margin of safety for this account while customer concentration remains this high.

## Conclusion

The 10-K strengthens CRDO's evidence quality but does not move CRDO into buy-zone eligibility. CRDO remains an active core candidate to monitor, not a current buy. A future promotion would require either a thesis-preserving valuation reset or stronger public evidence that growth durability and customer diversification offset the concentration risk.
