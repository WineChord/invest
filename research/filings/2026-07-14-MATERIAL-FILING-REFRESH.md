## Material decision updates

### MDA — offering closed; final optional dilution and acquisition execution remain

[MDA's July 14 Form 6-K exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1857047/000110465926083361/tm2619705d8_ex99-1.htm) confirms that the company closed the previously announced 23,000,000-share offering at USD 35.60 per share for about USD 818.8 million of gross proceeds. The July 9 prospectus capitalization table reported 138,659,395 shares before the offering and 161,659,395 after it. Underwriters retain a 30-day option for up to 3,450,000 additional shares, so fully exercised basic capitalization would reach 165,109,395 shares.

At the completed July 13 close of USD 31.47, basic market value is about USD 5.087 billion and would be about USD 5.195 billion after full option exercise. The offering close resolves one promotion-review trigger and improves funding certainty for CLS and Blue Canyon. It does not resolve the underwriter option, final acquisition financing and closing, post-deal leverage, integration, customer concentration, or the account's existing space concentration. MDA remains an active candidate and requires a current promotion/entry review; the filing alone does not create buy eligibility.

### CRCL — audited reserve-fund statements improve transparency, not entry economics

[Circle's July 13 Form 10-K/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1876042/000187604226000228/crcl-20251231.htm) was filed solely to add [audited April 30, 2026 financial statements for the Circle Reserve Fund](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1876042/000187604226000228/a43026auditedfinancialst.htm) under Regulation S-X Rule 3-09 and related certifications. Deloitte issued an unqualified opinion on those fund statements and did not audit internal control over financial reporting. The exhibit reported USD 66.367 billion of net assets, including USD 19.111 billion of Treasury bills and USD 46.998 billion of repurchase agreements, together representing 99.6% of net assets.

The amendment strengthens reserve-asset transparency and supports the reserve-quality evidence. It does not restate Circle's operating results, change distribution economics, prove ARC token shareholder capture, or reduce interest-rate and valuation risk. CRCL remains `watch` and outside a current buy zone.

## Issuer-release freshness extensions

### RKLB — VICTUS HAZE completed every phase faster than the government deadlines

[Rocket Lab's July 7 mission release](https://rocketlabcorp.com/updates/rocket-lab-delivers-mission-success-for-space-force-completes-historic-launch-and-on-orbit-satellite-tracking-mission-in-record-time/) reports that Electron launched after 16 hours and 42 minutes' notice, the Pioneer spacecraft was commissioned within 38 hours, and rendezvous and proximity operations were completed in less than 59 hours. Rocket Lab supplied launch, spacecraft, and mission operations as the single prime contractor. This is material positive execution evidence for responsive launch and integrated space systems, but it does not resolve Neutron, Iridium financing and integration, the USD 3 billion at-the-market program, valuation, or the account's 33.26% RKLB concentration. Hold 17 and buy zero.

### KTOS — approximately USD 500 million of fresh awards and funding strengthen demand evidence

[Kratos's July 13 release](https://www.kratosdefense.com/newsroom/kratos-receives-approximate-100-million-sole-source-prime-space-domain-awareness-system-program-award) reported an approximately USD 100 million sole-source production award for a ground-based modular space-domain-awareness system. A [July 14 company-distributed release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/14/3326764/224/en/Kratos-Receives-Approximately-400-Million-in-New-Funding-for-Hypersonic-System-and-Other-Programs.html) reported approximately USD 400 million of Department of War funding related to hypersonic and other national-security programs. The awards strengthen backlog and production-demand evidence. They do not change the current low operating margin, contractor economics, market scale, or account opportunity cost enough to pass the satellite entry gate. KTOS remains `watch`; buy zero.

### VOYG — Astrobotic closed and a new NASA lunar task order entered the acquired portfolio

[Voyager's July 13 release](https://voyagertechnologies.com/press-releases/voyager-awarded-298m-nasa-contract-under-completed-astrobotic-acquisition/) confirms the Astrobotic acquisition closed and the business now operates as Voyager Lunar Systems. NASA had awarded an approximately USD 298 million task order for two lunar lander missions, including Peregrine-2 planned for 2028, and Griffin Mission One was progressing toward a launch no earlier than November 2026. The close and task order strengthen lunar-platform scope. They also add mission-binary execution, integration, financing, margin, and stock-consideration risk to a business with negative gross profit and operating losses. VOYG remains `watch`; buy zero.

### AAOI — Pearland construction began, improving capacity evidence but increasing capital risk

[Applied Optoelectronics' July 14 release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/14/3326701/9986/en/aoi-begins-expansion-of-pearland-manufacturing-campus-to-scale-800g-and-1-6t-optical-transceiver-production.html) reports that construction began on two adjacent Pearland properties adding nearly 400,000 square feet of manufacturing capacity for 800G and 1.6T optical transceivers. The start converts a planned expansion into an execution milestone. It does not resolve customer concentration, current margins, cash conversion, build budget and timing, convertible debt, at-the-market issuance, customer-warrant dilution, or the demanding revenue multiple. AAOI remains research-only; buy zero.

### WYFI — pipeline claims and an R&D networking milestone do not replace audited conversion evidence

[WhiteFiber's June 10 investor presentation](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2042022/000121390026067329/ea029430601ex99-1.htm) described an approximately USD 865 million total contract value for the initial 40 MW NC-1 load, more than USD 175 million of recent cloud contract value, and a broader development pipeline. These are management presentation metrics, much of the economics are forward-looking, and NC-1 billing was subject to construction and commissioning. A [July company R&D release](https://www.whitefiber.com/blog/cross-data-center-networking-solution) reported 111.2 Tbps across 83 kilometers with 0.9 millisecond latency, while commercial availability, pricing, customer adoption, and revenue remained future work. The evidence improves technical and contract context but does not cure capital spending, convertible debt, customer-contract uncertainty, parent control, dilution, or valuation. WYFI remains research-only; buy zero.

### VRT — Malaysia capacity opened before the prior cutoff but needed durable classification

[Vertiv's July 1 company-distributed release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vertiv-increases-manufacturing-capacity-with-new-facility-in-malaysia-to-support-growing-demand-for-ai-and-digital-infrastructure-across-asia-302815814.html) announced a new Malaysia manufacturing facility supporting data-center and artificial-intelligence infrastructure demand in Asia. The opening strengthens manufacturing-capacity execution but predates the prior decision cutoff and is therefore missed historical evidence, not new July 14 information. It does not change Vertiv's roughly USD 117.5 billion scale, mature economics, AI capital-spending cyclicality, or the satellite account's extreme-asymmetry hurdle. VRT remains active; buy zero.

### IMSR — Texas A&M site agreements improve project formation, not project economics

[Terrestrial Energy's June 18 release](https://ir.terrestrialenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/terrestrial-energy-and-texas-am-university-system-sign) announced an exclusive 77-acre ground lease and research-and-development agreements at the Texas A&M RELLIS campus. The site supports characterization and environmental work for a future Nuclear Regulatory Commission construction-permit application, and the company opened a local office. This strengthens site-control and institutional-support evidence. Further board approvals, licensing, fuel, funded construction, customer commitments, and first-project economics remain unresolved. IMSR remains research-only; buy zero.

### POET — production and liquidity targets remain forward-looking issuer claims

[POET's June 30 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1437424/000117184326004397/exh_991.htm) targeted a second-half 2026 production ramp and capacity of up to one million units per month by the end of 2027. A [July 14 issuer FAQ](https://www.poet-technologies.com/blog/faq-production-orders-cash-position-and-the-path-to-greater-revenue) targeted more than 30,000 2026 shipments, described more than ten engagements expected to exceed USD 100 million of future annual revenue, projected about USD 50 million of second-half capital spending, and stated that June cash and short-term investments exceeded USD 830 million, with up to about USD 661 million of potential warrant proceeds. These unaudited, forward-looking statements improve scale and liquidity context but do not prove qualification, manufacturing yield, recognized revenue, gross margin, collections, or fully diluted value. POET remains research-only; buy zero.

## Financial-statement checklist

Only the CRCL amendment includes newly supplied audited financial statements, and those statements belong to the reserve fund rather than Circle's consolidated operating company. The review covered asset composition, net assets, audit opinion, and the limited purpose of the amendment. MDA, QBTS, CRDO, CRWV, RKLB, KTOS, VOYG, AAOI, VRT, WYFI, IMSR, and POET supplied no new complete quarterly financial statements in the events reviewed here. The issuer releases update execution, awards, transactions, capacity, technical claims, site formation, and management pipeline metrics; current completed filing and readiness reviews remain controlling for revenue, margins, cash flow, debt, dilution, and valuation.

## Conclusion

The MDA offering close is the only new event that directly reopens a current promotion and entry judgment. It improves financing certainty while leaving option dilution, two acquisition closings and integrations, final leverage, and portfolio concentration unresolved. RKLB, KTOS, and VOYG receive stronger operating or demand evidence; AAOI and VRT advance capacity execution; WYFI and POET add forward-looking contract, scale, technical, and liquidity context; IMSR improves site-control evidence; CRCL reserve transparency improves; QBTS's exchange transfer is administrative; and the CRDO and CRWV ownership filings reinforce existing entry discipline. None passes the mission, evidence, and entry gates for a new buy, and all reachable filing and issuer-release evidence identified by the freshness review is classified for this cycle.
