# NBIS July 2026 Facility and Q1 Metric Reconciliation

- Policy version: `v1.1`
- Review date: 2026-07-18
- Event severity: high
- Decision effect: thesis strengthened, capitalization corrected, entry still closed

## Review

Nebius disclosed a roughly USD 775 million senior secured facility priced at term SOFR plus 2.50%, maturing October 31, 2030. The facility is backed by deployed graphics processing units and contracted cash flows, contains liquidity and debt-service covenants, and is largely non-recourse to the parent subject to specified guarantees. Nebius says contracted customer cash flows plus the facility cover more than 100% of underlying GPU capital spending, the latest Microsoft capacity tranche was delivered on schedule, and more than USD 40 billion of additional customer commitments exist.

The repository's prior company-metrics row was stale for a foreign private issuer. The May 20 Q1 6-K reports USD 399.0 million Q1 revenue, USD 389.7 million Nebius AI cloud revenue, USD 174 million AI-cloud adjusted EBITDA, USD 9.298 billion of cash, and USD 2.473 billion of Q1 capital expenditures. It reports 220,406,311 outstanding Class A shares and 33,491,883 outstanding Class B shares after excluding treasury stock, or approximately 253.9 million basic economic shares, plus 21.066 million NVIDIA pre-funded warrants. At the USD 177.71 July 17 close, basic equity value is approximately USD 45.12 billion and basic plus pre-funded-warrant value is approximately USD 48.86 billion.

## Decision effect

The facility and Q1 growth strengthen operating proof and near-term funding. They do not clear customer concentration, utilization, capital intensity, secured leverage, convertible and pre-funded-warrant dilution, or valuation. Basic value is roughly 85 times trailing revenue; annualizing one high-growth quarter gives roughly 28 times basic sales and about 31 times basic-plus-prefunded sales, but that is a sensitivity rather than a forecast. NBIS remains `watch B+`, buy 0, pending a full promotion review and current capitalization reconciliation.
