## Filing Facts

Lumentum reported Q3 FY2026 revenue of USD 808.4 million, compared with USD 425.2 million in Q3 FY2025. Q3 GAAP gross profit was USD 357.0 million, GAAP operating income was USD 174.5 million, and GAAP net income was USD 144.2 million.

For the first nine months of FY2026, revenue was USD 2.0077 billion, gross profit was USD 778.6 million, operating income was USD 245.5 million, and net income was USD 226.6 million.

Cash and cash equivalents were USD 2.6178 billion at 2026-03-28. Current debt was USD 3.2386 billion and non-current debt was USD 43.2 million.

NVIDIA purchased approximately 2.9 million Series A Convertible Preferred shares for USD 2.0 billion in cash at USD 695.31 per share. The preferred shares convert one-for-one into common stock subject to the filing's HSR condition and related terms.

The 10-Q customer-concentration disclosure showed two customers above 10% of Q3 FY2026 sales, at 26% and 12%, and two customers above 10% of first-nine-month FY2026 sales, at 24% and 16%.

The April 2026 debt exchange issued common stock for portions of outstanding 2026 and 2029 notes and related conversion value. The 10-Q also disclosed remaining early conversion requests after quarter end. This makes capitalization and dilution review mandatory before any promotion.

Lumentum's Greensboro facility release describes a 240,000 square foot U.S. advanced-laser manufacturing facility expected to ramp in mid-2028. The facility strengthens the long-term InP laser capacity thesis, but its timing makes it insufficient for a current buy-zone upgrade.

The Nasdaq-100 inclusion is a liquidity and visibility event, not an independent business-quality upgrade.

## Thesis Impact

The filing package strengthens LITE as an AI optical and photonic interconnect watchlist candidate. The revenue step-up, margin expansion, cash raise, and NVIDIA relationship all indicate that Lumentum is closer to the AI optical bottleneck than a generic contract manufacturer or broad electronics supplier.

The filing package does not justify buy-zone promotion. The share price already embeds high expectations, and the capital structure is complex after the NVIDIA preferred-stock investment, 2032 notes issuance, and note-exchange activity. Customer concentration and optical-product commoditization risk remain material.

## Entry Impact

No buy-zone row should be created today. A buy review would require a thesis-preserving price dislocation, direct CPO or optical circuit switching revenue evidence, improved customer diversification, or clearer proof that NVIDIA-linked demand creates durable margin and pricing power.

## Risks To Monitor

- customer concentration and hyperscaler bargaining power;
- optical product commoditization and pricing pressure;
- debt, convertible-note, and preferred-stock dilution complexity;
- integration and execution after Cloud Light and the Greensboro manufacturing facility purchase;
- rare earth, tariff, export-control, and supply-chain risks;
- whether CPO and optical circuit switching become durable revenue rather than narrative optionality.
