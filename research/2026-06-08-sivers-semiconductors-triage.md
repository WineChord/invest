## Conclusion

Do not add SIVE or SIVEF to the eligible watchlist today.

Sivers Semiconductors AB is a real company with relevant AI optical interconnect, silicon photonics, mmWave, SATCOM, defense, and telecom exposure. The theme fits the semiconductor interconnect and memory lane better than most random community tickers. However, the currently visible securities do not fit this repository's policy v1.1 tradability boundary:

- SIVE is the Nasdaq Stockholm listing.
- SIVEF is the U.S. OTC ticker.
- Policy v1.1 excludes OTC securities and the operating process is currently built around U.S.-listed common stocks and ADRs with normal retail liquidity.

This is not a buy recommendation. The durable action is to record SIVEF as a rejected discovery candidate with a clear reopen condition: revisit only if Sivers completes a policy-eligible U.S. major-exchange listing or ADR and normal retail liquidity can be confirmed.

## Company Identity

Sivers Semiconductors AB describes itself as a photonics and wireless technology company. Its public materials point to two main technical areas:

- Photonics: DFB lasers and optical components for AI data-center optical interconnects, co-packaged optics, optical I/O, high-speed data links, sensing, and LiDAR.
- Wireless: RF beamformer and mmWave technologies for SATCOM, fixed wireless access, defense, telecom, and 5G or 6G applications.

The company reports under the SIVE ticker on Nasdaq Stockholm. Third-party market data identifies SIVEF as the OTC Markets ticker for the same issuer.

## Why The Theme Is Interesting

The bottleneck question is whether AI data-center scaling makes optical bandwidth, power-efficient interconnect, light engines, and laser-array supply more scarce. Sivers has relevant primary-source evidence:

- On 2026-06-02, Sivers announced a collaboration with GlobalFoundries to integrate Sivers laser arrays into GF silicon photonics reference designs and SCALE optical engine solutions for AI infrastructure.
- On 2026-04-15, Sivers announced a collaboration with Jabil on a 1.6T linear receive optical transceiver module using Sivers DFB lasers.
- Sivers' own AI/data communications application page frames its DFB lasers as inputs for CPO, optical I/O, AI accelerators, data centers, and HPC clusters.

Those facts make it a legitimate lane signal. They do not solve the tradability problem.

## Current Evidence And Risks

Primary evidence is mixed and early:

- Q1 2026 net sales were SEK 61.9 million, down 22% year over year.
- Q1 2026 adjusted EBITDA was negative SEK 13.8 million, and operating cash flow was negative SEK 49.2 million.
- The company highlighted opportunity pipeline growth, multiple planned ramps into 2027, a directed share issue of about SEK 125 million, debt refinancing, and preparation for a potential U.S. dual listing.
- The 2026 AGM notice authorizes potential issuance of up to 53,844,956 ordinary shares, about 15.0% dilution after dilution, partly to support a possible Nasdaq New York dual listing.
- The same AGM notice references a secured convertible loan with Bootstrap Europe at a 10.85% fixed annual interest rate.

The business may be strategically interesting, but the security still carries early commercialization, customer-conversion, financing, dilution, foreign-listing, OTC-liquidity, and volatility risk.
