# Research

This directory stores durable research memory.

Use it to avoid repeating low-value searches, but never treat it as fresh market
truth. Every monthly decision must refresh current prices, company disclosures,
regulatory updates, and material news.

Files:

- `watchlist.csv`: current candidate universe and monitoring status.
- `sources.yml`: durable source register.
- `2026-05-26-initial-baseline.md`: first baseline research snapshot.

Future company-specific files should follow
[templates/company-research-card.md](templates/company-research-card.md).
