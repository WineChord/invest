# FN Watchlist Triage

Policy version: v1.1

Review date: 2026-05-31

Ticker reviewed: FN, Fabrinet.

Conclusion: add FN to `research/watchlist.csv` only as `research_only` with priority `C+`. This is not a buy recommendation and not an active-candidate promotion.

## Bottleneck Fit

```yaml
what_could_become_scarce: High-reliability optical packaging, datacom optical manufacturing, datacenter interconnect assembly, and high-performance computing electro-mechanical manufacturing capacity could become constrained if AI data-center buildouts continue to require faster, denser, and more power-efficient connectivity.
who_controls_or_removes_scarcity: Fabrinet operates advanced optical packaging and precision manufacturing capacity for complex OEM products, but customers and component designers still appear to own much of the product architecture and end-demand leverage.
who_can_monetize_into_shareholder_value: Fabrinet can monetize the bottleneck through volume growth, yield, capacity utilization, and operating discipline; it has less obvious pricing power than IP-heavy interconnect companies because the model is closer to specialized contract manufacturing.
public_security_expression: FN is a NYSE-listed common stock with SEC reporting history under CIK 0001408710.
early_small_misunderstood_or_newly_public: FN is public, profitable, and already a mid-cap/large-cap supplier rather than a newly public asymmetric option. Its research value is as an optical-manufacturing bottleneck monitor and peer, not as a current first-buy candidate.
```

## Fresh Evidence

Fabrinet's fiscal Q3 2026 release, published 2026-05-04 and retrieved 2026-05-31, reported record quarterly revenue of USD 1.214 billion, up from USD 871.8 million in the prior-year quarter, GAAP net income of USD 125.2 million, and Q4 revenue guidance of USD 1.25 billion to USD 1.29 billion.

The fiscal Q3 2026 Form 10-Q, filed 2026-05-05 and retrieved 2026-05-31, supports the AI optical relevance but also explains the promotion limit. Q3 optical communications revenue was about USD 888.7 million, including about USD 260.4 million of datacom and about USD 196.9 million of datacenter interconnect revenue. Non-optical communications revenue included about USD 106.7 million of high-performance computing revenue. The same filing's XBRL facts show four major customers represented 58.1% of revenue for the nine months ended 2026-03-27.

Yahoo Finance chart data retrieved 2026-05-31 showed a 2026-05-29 close near USD 654.16. The refreshed SEC companyfacts row implies about USD 23.4 billion of market capitalization, about 5.6x TTM sales, about 56x trailing earnings, and about 11.9% TTM gross margin. At that price, FN has already repriced around strong AI optical demand, so the issue is not whether the business is improving; it is whether the equity offers enough long-term asymmetric upside versus existing interconnect names.

## Decision

FN clears a low-priority research-only gate because it is more directly tied to AI optical/datacom manufacturing capacity than the broad industrial list where it was previously rejected. It does not clear a buy gate because the company appears to be a specialized manufacturing services supplier with low gross margin, customer concentration, limited visible pricing power, and less extreme upside than CRDO or ALAB if the AI interconnect bottleneck persists.

Next review trigger: next quarterly report or customer/datacom/HPC concentration update.
