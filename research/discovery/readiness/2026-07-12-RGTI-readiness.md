## Bottleneck Fit

Rigetti directly controls a full-stack superconducting quantum platform, including its Fab-1 manufacturing capability and the generally available 108-qubit Cepheus-1-108Q modular system. The scarce capability is not raw qubit count; it is repeatable high-fidelity system delivery, customer access, useful workloads, and a roadmap that survives rapid competing modalities. Rigetti is a U.S.-listed common stock and a direct exposure, but current commercial proof is too small to show durable pricing power or shareholder value capture.

## Analysis

The company is more direct and commercially tangible than several keyword-only quantum leads, but about USD 5.55 billion of market value against USD 4.4 million of quarterly revenue leaves almost no entry margin for technical or commercial disappointment. Cash runway reduces near-term ruin risk but does not solve dilution, rapid obsolescence, government/customer concentration, or the possibility that useful fault-tolerant workloads arrive later than the capital market assumes. IQMX has stronger delivered-system revenue, IONQ has broader commercial scale, and QBTS has annealing bookings; all remain expensive, so adding Rigetti improves lane completeness rather than buy eligibility.
