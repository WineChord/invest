## Conclusion

Optical Cable Corporation is a material small-cap optical-connectivity comparator and is not rejected as noise. It remains `research_only`: buy 0 at the July 15 completed close of USD 16.36. Sales, backlog, quarterly profit, and gross margin improved at a low headline sales multiple, but AI and data-center exposure is unquantified and the cash, operating-cash-flow, secured-revolver, customer, working-capital, and moat risks fail the evidence and survival gates.

## Bottleneck and mission fit

OCC manufactures fiber-optic cabling and connectivity products for enterprise, data-center, and severe-duty applications. The company is much smaller than LITE, AAOI, FN, and other optical comparators, so successful niche pricing or data-center wins could matter. Current filings, however, do not quantify data-center revenue, orders, market share, technical differentiation, or durable pricing power. Mission fit is therefore conditional rather than proven.

## Primary evidence

The June 8 Q2 2026 Form 10-Q reported quarterly sales of USD 22.214 million, up 26.6%, and first-half sales of USD 38.645 million, up 16.1%. Q2 gross margin was 34.2%, quarterly net income was USD 1.055 million, and first-half net income was USD 0.657 million. Backlog and forward load rose to USD 13.3 million from USD 10.4 million at January 31 and USD 7.3 million at October 31. One distributor represented 16.9% of Q2 sales and 16.5% of first-half sales.

Liquidity is the decisive risk. Cash was approximately USD 0.146 million, first-half operating cash flow was negative USD 1.4 million, the revolver had approximately USD 7.3 million outstanding and USD 4.6 million available at an 8.25% rate, and term debt was approximately USD 2.65 million. The revolver matures July 24, 2027, has broad collateral, and includes subjective acceleration provisions.

Trailing revenue was approximately USD 78.390 million. The June 2 filing-cover count was 8,827,092 shares; at USD 16.36, basic market value was approximately USD 144.4 million, roughly 1.84 times trailing sales. The apparently low multiple does not compensate for unproved AI exposure and fragile liquidity without positive cash conversion.

## Decision gates

- Mission: conditional pass. Optical connectivity is relevant, but bottleneck control is unproved.
- Evidence: pass for research, fail for a buy. Growth and backlog are real; data-center mix and durable moat are not quantified.
- Entry: superficially attractive, still fail. Low sales multiple is offset by liquidity and working-capital risk.
- Survival: fail for new capital. Thin cash, negative operating cash flow, secured revolver dependence, and a 2027 maturity reduce failure tolerance.
- Opportunity cost: AAOI, FN, LITE, and POET offer clearer sector exposure or stronger balance sheets; cash retains option value.

## Triggers and invalidation

Reopen after named or quantified data-center wins, sustained backlog conversion and gross margin above 30%, positive operating cash flow, higher cash, revolver de-risking, and clean share-count disclosure. Reject or demote if backlog reverses, margin retraces, revolver availability or covenants tighten, subjective acceleration risk rises, operating cash flow stays negative, or dilution becomes the liquidity cure.
