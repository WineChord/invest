## Bottleneck Fit

NWPX manufactures large engineered steel and concrete water-transmission systems and reinforced precast products. The company operates regional manufacturing capacity rather than a software platform, so the potential scarcity lies in qualified physical capacity, project execution, logistics, and installed relationships. EPA's Water Reuse Action Plan identifies semiconductor fabrication and data-center cooling as reuse applications, but that policy context is not a mandate and does not prove NWPX-specific demand.
