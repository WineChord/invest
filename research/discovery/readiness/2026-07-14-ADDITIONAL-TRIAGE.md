# Additional Discovery Triage — 2026-07-14

KRMN, QNC, and ARQQ received bounded primary-source reviews so they do not recycle as untriaged community or deterministic leads. They are not material enough to require research-only dashboard coverage this cycle.

## KRMN

Karman reported Q1 revenue of USD 151.210 million, net income of USD 7.8 million, adjusted EBITDA of USD 44.8 million, and about USD 1.0 billion of backlog. Cash was USD 73.798 million against about USD 855.538 million of debt; operating cash flow was only USD 0.209 million, and two customers represented 25.6% and 11.6% of revenue. The company is a credible operating space supplier, but leverage, cash conversion, concentration, acquisition integration, and stronger existing lane coverage make it not material to current allocation. Buy zero; reopen after debt reduction, free-cash-flow proof, lower concentration and integration risk, and a material valuation reset.

## QNC

Quantum eMotion reported Q1 revenue of only CAD 10,582 and a CAD 3.588 million net loss. Current and strategic investments were about CAD 39.9 million, while the SecureKey acquisition includes earnouts and royalties. The issuer is technically relevant to post-quantum security, but distribution, recurring revenue, acquisition economics, burn, dilution, and current value remain unproved. Defer as not material; buy zero; reopen after meaningful recurring revenue and distribution proof.

## ARQQ

Arqit's first-half filing reported USD 623,000 of revenue, USD 28.9 million of cash, and USD 33.9 million of six-month administrative expense. Direct quantum-safe technology has not converted into commercial scale or credible per-share economics. Reject as not material; buy zero; reopen only after orders-of-magnitude revenue improvement, materially lower burn, stronger runway, and a major valuation reset.
