## Evidence and decision

AXT supplies indium-phosphide and other compound-semiconductor substrates used in high-speed optical connectivity. Q2 revenue was USD 47.6 million, up 164.8% year over year, gross margin reached 44.9%, operating profit was USD 10.4 million, and estimated Q2 operating cash flow less capital spending was positive USD 3.6 million. No customer exceeded 10% of revenue and the top five represented about 30%–31%, improving the customer-quality evidence. The six-year Lumentum capacity agreement includes annual minimum purchases or shortfall payments and an initial USD 43.5 million deposit.

The per-share entry case still fails. China was the destination for about 65% of revenue, U.S. indium-phosphide export permission remains blocked, and the manufacturing and ownership structure retains geopolitical risk. The August 13 completed close was USD 77.45; a later event-day snapshot near USD 84.16 was not a completed close. Using roughly 67.8 million gross diluted shares, annualized EV/sales was approximately 24.5x–26.9x, which already capitalizes a large part of the operating inflection.
