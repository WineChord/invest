## Bottleneck Fit

Quantum-Si sells protein-sequencing instruments, consumables, software access, maintenance and training. Its planned Proteus platform is intended to increase throughput, automate workflows and provide single-molecule amino-acid-level measurements. If the platform works and creates a recurring consumables installed base, it could lower a scarce biological-measurement constraint and support attractive platform economics. That result is not yet demonstrated.

The May 7 Form 10-Q reports first-quarter revenue of USD 0.258 million, down 69.4 percent, gross profit of USD 0.074 million, a USD 21.7 million net loss, and USD 25.6 million of operating cash use. Cash, cash equivalents and current and non-current marketable securities totaled USD 190.4 million. As of May 4, 217.8 million Class A and Class B shares were outstanding, and another 44.4 million options, RSUs and warrants were excluded from diluted EPS.

Management attributes part of the legacy revenue decline to customers delaying purchases before the planned year-end Proteus launch. That explanation is plausible but not validated demand. At the July 22 completed close of USD 0.80, basic equity value is approximately USD 174 million using the May 4 share count, slightly below disclosed liquidity before future burn, liabilities and dilution. The apparent discount creates optionality, not a current buy: roughly USD 25.6 million of quarterly operating cash use can consume the balance-sheet cushion quickly.

## Decision

QSI enters C+ research-only coverage with quantity zero and no executable limit or target. Reopen promotion only after Proteus launches with independently validated performance, paid placements, recurring consumables demand, improving gross margin, a controlled cash-use path and a reconciled diluted share count. A lower price alone is not sufficient.
