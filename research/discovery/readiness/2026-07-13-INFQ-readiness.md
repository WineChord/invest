## Bottleneck Fit

Infleqtion directly develops neutral-atom quantum computers, atomic clocks, radio-frequency sensing, inertial and resilient Positioning, Navigation, and Timing systems, and enabling software. The potentially scarce capability is not the word quantum; it is field-qualified atomic systems and useful compute or sensing that can operate where satellite navigation, conventional sensing, or classical compute is inadequate.

- what_could_become_scarce: Deployable quantum sensing, resilient PNT, radio-frequency detection, and useful neutral-atom compute.
- who_controls_or_removes_scarcity: Infleqtion controls integrated atomic hardware, software, government-qualified delivery experience, and neutral-atom technical roadmaps.
- who_can_monetize_into_shareholder_value: The company can create durable value only if commercial revenue, gross margin, and recurring performance obligations compound faster than burn and dilution.
- public_security_expression: NYSE common stock INFQ is direct and policy-eligible; INFQ warrants are not eligible under policy v1.1.
- early_small_misunderstood_or_newly_public: INFQ began public trading in February 2026 and has more operating evidence than a pre-revenue quantum story, but the current capitalization already assumes large commercialization success.

## Risks and Kill Criteria

- 2026 revenue materially misses USD 40 million.
- Gross margin remains below 25% without a credible mix explanation or fails to recover above 35% over time.
- Government concentration stays above 80% while a major program expires without replacement.
- Share count expands more than 15% without proportionate per-share value creation.
- Annualized operating cash burn rises above USD 100 million.
- Defined technical milestones slip without an evidence-based roadmap revision.
- The conditional CHIPS support fails to become definitive or creates disproportionate dilution.
