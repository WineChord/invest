## Evidence and decision

Red Cat has direct defense-drone and autonomy exposure and provides a necessary peer test for ONDS. Q1 revenue was USD 15.5 million. Organic product demand is more tangible than many autonomy leads, but customer concentration, acquisition effects, material control weaknesses and repeated equity financing remain material. A May offering added 23.9 million shares plus a 3.6 million-share option.
