## Analysis

The product direction is real, but the current price already capitalizes a major 800G and later 1.6T ramp. A public market-data provider reported about USD 9.1 billion market value, while applying the USD 119.92 close to the 80.24 million May 5 filing share count yields about USD 9.6 billion; neither basis leaves a compelling entry margin. Customer concentration, 29% gross margins, negative operating cash conversion, large capacity spending, ATM issuance, convertible debt, and customer-warrant dilution weaken shareholder capture. CRDO and ALAB offer higher-margin bottleneck economics, LITE offers laser and optical-circuit-switch exposure, and FN provides manufacturing comparison at a lower multiple.
