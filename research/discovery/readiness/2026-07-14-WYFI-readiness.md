## Bottleneck Fit

The potentially scarce capability is deployable AI-compute capacity with power, data-center control, customer delivery, and financing that survives hardware depreciation. WhiteFiber directly combines GPU cloud services and colocation through owned and managed data centers. It can create shareholder value only if utilization, contract quality, margins, and cash generation compound faster than debt, capital spending, and dilution. Nasdaq common stock WYFI is policy-eligible. The company completed its initial public offering in August 2025 and is smaller and less seasoned than CRWV, NBIS, and IREN, but its current valuation already assumes substantial success.

## Risks and Kill Criteria

- Q2 revenue or deferred-revenue conversion weakens without a timing explanation.
- The paused customer relationship causes a material write-off, refund, or idle-capacity period.
- Accounts receivable grows faster than collected revenue for two consecutive quarters.
- Annualized capital spending remains above revenue without funded, contracted, high-return capacity.
- Net debt rises while adjusted EBITDA falls or conversion dilution expands materially.
- Bit Digital related-party terms or overlapping management impair minority-shareholder economics.

## Decision

WYFI improves AI-compute lane completeness and receives research-only dashboard visibility at C+. It is not eligible for a new buy because valuation, operating losses, capital intensity, convertible debt, customer-contract uncertainty, parent control, and dilution leave insufficient margin for the satellite mission. Buy zero shares and sell zero shares. Reopen after the Q2 filing, contract-resolution evidence, deferred-revenue conversion, better free-cash-flow visibility, less fragile financing, minority-shareholder governance improvement, or a material valuation reset.
