## Bottleneck Fit

Hyperscale Data combines bitcoin mining and a diversified legacy group with a planned data-center services deployment. The signed agreement makes the exposure real enough to review, but ownership of a small amount of prospective capacity does not establish scarce, financed and profitable infrastructure.

## Evidence Gathered

The definitive agreement provides 20 MW in two phases, a USD 5.6 million security deposit and a USD 5 million upfront charge. Against that, the Q1 filing reports a USD 30.1 million net loss, weighted shares of 380.7 million versus 6.3 million a year earlier, another 91.1 million shares sold through May 15 and a subsequent USD 300 million ATM.
