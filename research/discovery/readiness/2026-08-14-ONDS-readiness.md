## Evidence and decision

The August 13 Form 10-Q confirms a real autonomous-defense and counter-UAS operating base, but it does not yet establish clean per-share value capture. Q2 revenue was USD 83.8 million, while five acquisitions contributed about 38% of reported revenue. Management separately described organic growth of 85%; the pro-forma comparison implies a materially different rate and remains unreconciled. H1 operating cash use was USD 137.4 million, with estimated Q2 operating cash use near USD 86.1 million and operating cash flow less capital spending near negative USD 93.8 million.

The capitalization remains the controlling blocker. Basic shares reached about 570.6 million by August 11, and a conservative lower-bound bridge including disclosed warrants, options, RSUs and legal rights approaches 881 million potential shares. Liquidity lowers immediate survival risk, but acquisition accounting, cash burn and the denominator prevent an R2 comparison or promotion.
