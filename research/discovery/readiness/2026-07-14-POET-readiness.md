## Bottleneck Fit

POET develops optical engines, modules, and light-source products based on a patented wafer-level Optical Interposer platform. If qualification and manufacturing scale work, the technology could reduce cost, size, and power while addressing bandwidth and latency constraints in artificial-intelligence clusters. POET therefore directly improves the optical-interconnect lane rather than serving as a generic semiconductor proxy. Nasdaq common stock is policy-eligible, but manufacturing partners, customer qualification, production yield, customer credit, and capital structure control shareholder value capture.

- what_could_become_scarce: Qualified, low-power, high-bandwidth optical engines that can be manufactured at AI-cluster scale.
- who_controls_or_removes_scarcity: POET controls the interposer design while customers, foundry and assembly partners, qualification, and manufacturing yield control commercial conversion.
- who_can_monetize_into_shareholder_value: Common holders benefit only if production orders become recognized high-margin revenue and collected cash faster than capital spending and dilution.

## Evidence Gathered

POET reported Q1 revenue of only USD 503,389, a USD 12.344 million net loss, and USD 8.8 million of operating cash use. At March 31, cash was USD 16.537 million and short-term investments were USD 412.599 million. The Lumilens agreement included an initial USD 50 million purchase order and described potential cumulative purchases above USD 500 million over five years, but development, qualification, manufacturing scale, timing, margins, customer credit, cancellation, and cash-collection terms are not fully disclosed. The related warrant can cover up to about 22.92 million shares.

The May financing issued 19,047,620 common shares plus warrants for another 19,047,620 shares at a combined USD 21.00 price and expected about USD 399.676 million of net proceeds. The prospectus used 153,547,786 pre-offering shares, implying 172,595,406 post-offering basic shares. It separately disclosed 37,364,941 pre-existing warrants, 5,791,997 options, and 3,262,707 restricted stock units, before the new offering warrant and Lumilens warrant. Fully diluted exposure therefore exceeds 250 million shares before any later changes.

The completed July 13 close was USD 8.00. It is a research snapshot, not an executable quote. The SEC post-offering basic count implies about USD 1.381 billion of basic equity value, not the approximately USD 520 million vendor figure observed during the review. Post-offering liquid resources may be substantial, but they cannot be treated as confirmed current cash until the next balance sheet, and the layered capital structure makes an unreconciled vendor market capitalization unusable.

A June 30 issuer release targeted a production ramp in the second half of 2026 and capacity of up to one million units per month by the end of 2027. A July 14 issuer FAQ added a target of more than 30,000 2026 shipments, more than ten engagements that management expects could exceed USD 100 million of future annual revenue, about USD 50 million of second-half capital spending, more than USD 830 million of June cash and short-term investments, and up to about USD 661 million of potential warrant proceeds. These are material forward-looking issuer statements rather than audited conversion evidence. They strengthen scale and liquidity context but do not prove qualification, orders, manufacturing yield, recognized revenue, margin, cash collection, or fully diluted shareholder economics.

## Risks and Kill Criteria

- Lumilens qualification, order conversion, or collected cash fails to appear on the expected schedule.
- Production yield, cost, or manufacturing capacity prevents gross-margin scaling.
- A customer or partner dispute exposes weak cancellation or credit protections.
- Fully diluted share growth outruns commercial revenue and cash conversion.
- Competing optical integration reaches comparable cost, power, and scale first.

## Decision

POET receives research-only dashboard visibility at C+. It is not promoted and is not buy-eligible. Buy zero shares and sell zero shares. Reopen after customer qualification, visible order conversion, manufacturing yield and margin proof, fully reconciled capitalization, disciplined cash deployment, and an attractive fully diluted valuation.
