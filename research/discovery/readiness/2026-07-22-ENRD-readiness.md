## Bottleneck Fit

The scarce capability is not an electric truck by itself. It is the integrated ability to plan, deploy, supervise, insure, and operate autonomous freight safely across real customer routes while coordinating vehicles, charging, software, and remote operations. Einride combines Freight-Capacity-as-a-Service with its Saga fleet platform and Einride Driver autonomous stack. It can create exceptional per-share value only if software and autonomous licensing become higher-margin, repeatable economics rather than an asset-heavy transport service that consumes capital faster than it scales. Nasdaq ADSs under ENRD are policy-eligible and began trading on June 10, 2026.

## Risks and Kill Criteria

- Going-concern risk persists after post-closing proceeds or unrestricted cash falls below twelve months of credible operating and deployment needs.
- Gross margin remains negative while revenue grows, showing that scale does not improve freight economics.
- Customer concentration rises, another large customer terminates, or RPO fails to convert into collected revenue.
- Autonomous deployments, safety approvals, uptime, or commercial licensing milestones slip materially.
- Warrants, resale supply, equity incentives, or new financing transfer most upside away from current ADS holders.
- Trading liquidity remains too thin for disciplined entry or exit.

## Decision

ENRD is a material direct autonomous-freight expression and belongs in research-only coverage at C+. It is not buy-ready. Current action is zero shares with no limit price. Reconsider only after a post-closing filing reconciles unrestricted cash and diluted capitalization, gross margin improves, RPO converts, customer concentration falls, autonomous deployments produce repeatable commercial revenue, and liquidity becomes adequate.
