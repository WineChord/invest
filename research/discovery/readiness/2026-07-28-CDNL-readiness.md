## Bottleneck Fit

Cardinal performs wet-utility installation, grading, site clearing, paving and other civil work in the southeastern United States. Its in-house crews and equipment can benefit from infrastructure demand, including large commercial and industrial projects, but the filing does not establish ownership of a scarce network, proprietary technology or durable pricing control.
