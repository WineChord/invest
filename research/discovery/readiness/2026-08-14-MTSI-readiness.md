## Evidence and decision

MACOM is a direct RF, microwave, optical and photonic-semiconductor supplier to data-center, telecom, industrial and defense systems. Fiscal Q3 revenue was USD 342.2 million and data-center revenue was USD 137.6 million, establishing real operating scale and directness. The company is a useful same-lane comparison for CRDO, LITE, COHR, LASR and AAOI rather than a weak infrastructure proxy.

At the August 13 close of USD 307.06, the independently reconciled diluted enterprise value was about USD 23.8 billion, or roughly 17.4x annualized revenue and about 115x annualized free cash flow. A stage-adjusted review range of USD 144–214 per share, with USD 266 only as a stretch case, leaves insufficient entry margin. Net debt, convertible claims, equity awards and customer concentration reinforce the valuation objection.
