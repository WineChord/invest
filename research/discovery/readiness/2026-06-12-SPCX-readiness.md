## Decision Impact

SPCX is important enough to monitor immediately, but it is not buy-ready. First-day mega-cap valuation, controlled-company governance, xAI/X loss burden, Starship and Starlink execution risk, large potential stock-funded transactions, equity-plan capacity, and IPO volatility all block buy eligibility.

SPCX also raises the opportunity-cost bar for RKLB and ASTS. It does not break either thesis today, but it reduces the value of assuming that smaller public space names are the only direct public expressions of the bottleneck.

## Analysis

SPCX has the strongest absolute business scope in the lane map, but the public-market entry is too uncertain. The first-day valuation is already around USD 2.1 trillion, while the prospectus shows significant xAI/X losses and controlled-company governance. The candidate is therefore useful as an opportunity-cost benchmark and competitor signal, not as a current buy.
