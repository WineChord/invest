## Bottleneck Fit

Industrial process heat and microreactor fuel could be scarce, but NuCube is a transaction-stage expression rather than an operating listed security.

## Evidence Gathered

The September 11 S-4 reports no commercial revenue, no approved NuSun site and an estimated roughly USD 150 million FOAK capital requirement. The September 29 Form 425 is marketing, not a new operating milestone.

## Analysis

Authorization, fuel, customer economics, funding and final ownership are decision-critical for this candidate. Waiting until October 2 can miss a transaction rerating but avoids treating an unlisted, unfunded concept as an account alternative.

## Decision

R1 researchable and incubated under a dated SLA. No promotion, buy zone or account action. Advance only on confirmed security eligibility plus bounded authorization, funding and customer economics.
