## Conclusion

ALP reported that a 504-GPU NVIDIA B200 cluster was operational and supported by a two-year USD 32.2 million off-take contract, with a second 576-GPU B300 cluster planned. That is direct enough to warrant review, but not durable coverage today.

## Bottleneck Fit

- `what_could_become_scarce`: operating GPU clusters with power, networking, commissioning, and credible customer demand.
- `who_controls_or_removes_scarcity`: ALP reports control of one B200 cluster and a planned B300 cluster.
- `who_can_monetize_into_shareholder_value`: only auditable utilization, contract protection, gross margin, cash conversion, and survivable financing would preserve economics for common shareholders.
- `public_security_expression`: Nasdaq-listed ALP is a direct security, but listing alone does not create buy eligibility.
- `early_small_misunderstood_or_newly_public`: the compute pivot is small and awkward, but complexity currently reflects risk rather than demonstrated mispricing.

## Evidence Gathered

For the year ended March 31, 2026, the issuer reported an approximately USD 38.63 million net loss, approximately USD 18.5 million operating cash use, negative working capital of approximately USD 8.74 million, and substantial doubt about continuing as a going concern. July cash of approximately USD 10.3 million was stated to be insufficient for twelve months. A USD 40.9 million server purchase, approximately USD 8.6 million of deposits, approximately USD 29.6 million of lease exposure, an unnamed customer, incomplete contract protections and utilization economics, unrelated legacy and GAMEE activity, related-party complexity, and likely additional financing prevent a reliable per-share return case.

Required surfaces: `security_metadata` confirms current public listing; `market_data` was reviewed only for bounded triage; `filings_and_reports` include the annual and issuer-furnished update; `same_lane_peers` include IREN, NBIS, CRWV, CIFR, SEI, and cash.

## Analysis

The reported contract could matter, but counterparty quality, enforceability, termination, utilization, pricing, energy, colocation, lease, and depreciation economics are not sufficient to separate a valuable cluster from a financing-dependent asset bundle. Existing compute candidates have stronger audited operating or funding evidence.

## Decision

The result is `rejected_after_review`, buy zero, with no dashboard surface required. Reopen only after audited cluster-level revenue, enforceable and creditworthy customer disclosure, utilization and gross-margin proof, a survivable post-financing capital structure, separation from unrelated activities, and several quarters of cash conversion.
