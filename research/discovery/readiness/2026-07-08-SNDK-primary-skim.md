## Why Reviewed

The 2026-07-08 public community triage surfaced SNDK with enough attention to justify a primary-source skim before the monthly allocation decision. The objective was to decide whether SNDK should become an allocation-relevant raw candidate or displace the current semiconductor memory watch lane.

## Evidence

The primary filing confirms SNDK is a real Nasdaq-listed flash-memory issuer spun out of Western Digital. The business has relevant storage and NAND-cycle exposure, but the current skim also shows Flash Ventures obligations, NAND/storage cyclicality, and Western Digital secondary-sale overhang context.

## Decision

SNDK is not material to the current allocation decision. It does not displace MU as the existing memory-cycle watch name and does not compete with RKLB or ASTS for current confirmed cash. No candidate-readiness record is required unless later primary evidence shows a clearer bottleneck-control path, better asymmetry, or a thesis-preserving price dislocation.
