## Bottleneck Fit

Quantum Cyber describes autonomy software with an optimization layer that can submit work to cloud quantum services and integrate third-party quantum sensors. That architecture may use scarce technology, but the company does not show ownership of the underlying quantum hardware or a paid, repeatable autonomy platform.

## Evidence Gathered

The May 26 Form 10-Q reports USD 4.75 million cash at March 31 and a USD 5.08 million quarterly net loss. It also records authority for a reverse split and an increase to 900 million authorized ordinary shares. The June 2 business update describes an optimization interface to external quantum resources and future sensor deployments, but does not provide recognized recurring platform revenue, independent product validation or control of the underlying quantum hardware.
