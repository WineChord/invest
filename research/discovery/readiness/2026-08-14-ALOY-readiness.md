## Evidence and decision

ALLOY targets a North American mine-to-NdFeB-magnet chain and is therefore a more direct strategic-materials expression than a generic miner. Q1 revenue was only USD 0.7 million, ongoing positive operating cash flow was absent and stock compensation was USD 81.8 million. A June financing added about 7.0 million shares for USD 100 million gross proceeds. The commercial base, governance, financing and per-share capture remain unproved.
