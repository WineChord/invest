## Bottleneck Fit

WaterBridge owns and operates produced-water handling, recycling and brackish-water infrastructure in the Permian Basin. A scaled network with minimum-volume commitments can be a scarce local asset, but its demand remains tied to oil and gas development, customer drilling plans, disposal regulation and environmental liability rather than the broader water-transmission bottleneck represented by NWPX.

## Evidence Gathered

The March-quarter filing reports USD 201.0 million of revenue, up 105% year over year, USD 102.9 million of adjusted EBITDA and USD 48.2 million of GAAP gross margin. It also reports USD 1.49 billion of total debt, a multi-entity Up-C-style ownership and noncontrolling-interest structure, related-party arrangements, substantial capital requirements and environmental and commodity-cycle exposure. As of May 6, Class A plus Class B economic interests totaled 123,456,209; at the July 27 completed close of USD 33.34, the basic economic value is about USD 4.12 billion before preferred units, incentive awards and other structural claims.
