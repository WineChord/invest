At USD 20.61, 83,845,141 current economic shares imply approximately USD 1.728 billion of market value. Treating the full 9,214,301-share incentive-plan reserve as a conservative dilution envelope produces approximately USD 1.918 billion, although the reserve is not current issuance. The company reports no warrants after the August 20 redemption and approximately USD 130 million of aggregate gross warrant proceeds, but current unrestricted cash after expansion spending is unavailable; a current EV is therefore N/A rather than an asynchronous mix of June cash and August shares. Current market value is about 13.9 times annualized Q2 revenue and about 4.3 times management's unproved USD 400 million exit-ARR target.

The bull path requires the USD 1.9 billion TCV to convert, new sites to energize on time and 15–20 percent net cash flow margin to persist after lease principal and customer-deposit normalization. The bear path is that deposits finance a commitment-heavy buildout whose depreciation, colocation, software and financing costs leave little residual common-equity cash. IREN presently supplies stronger accepted-deployment evidence; APLD, CRWV and NBIS show how financing and concentration can dominate otherwise strong demand. Waiting risks missing a rerating, but buying after a 13.30 percent one-day increase without full-cost economics creates a larger false-positive risk.
