## Evidence and decision

nLIGHT supplies high-power semiconductor and fiber lasers and develops directed-energy systems, giving the company direct exposure to scarce photonics and defense capabilities. Q2 revenue was USD 82.6 million, 69% came from aerospace and defense, H1 operating cash flow was positive USD 30.4 million, and cash was USD 296 million. The smaller denominator and defense programs preserve a plausible right tail.

At the August 13 close of USD 50.35, diluted enterprise value was approximately USD 2.84 billion. Current evidence does not provide a usable backlog bridge; Q3 product revenue near USD 17 million is supply constrained, development gross margin is only about 6%, and customer and program timing remain material. A stage-adjusted review range of USD 26–37, with USD 47 as a stretch case, does not support entry at the completed close.
