## Evidence and decision

BRUN is a newly public GPU-cloud and colocation operator with actual revenue and a small enough denominator to warrant false-negative protection. Q1 revenue was USD 11.0 million, net loss USD 4.1 million and operating cash flow USD 13.2 million. After its transaction, the company had about 31.9 million Class A and 29.5 million Class B shares before earnouts and warrants. Management separately described USD 940 million of contracted customer revenue, but that claim is not yet reconciled to audited backlog, cancellation rights, customer concentration, delivery obligations or full-cost economics.
