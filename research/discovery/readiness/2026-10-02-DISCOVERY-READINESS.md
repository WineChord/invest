### LPTH: corrected denominator and falsifiable value bridge

The FY26 10-K cover states **70,036,252 shares outstanding on September 8, 2026**, not 39.939m. June 30 shares were 69,963,045 versus 42,949,307 a year earlier. Root independently retrieved the **October 1 completed close of $10.85** from Yahoo chart data and excluded the incomplete October 2 session. Basic market capitalization is therefore **$759.893m**, before preferred conversion, awards, options and acquisition consideration. The $433m calculation using 39.939m shares is rejected.

The filing reports no warrants outstanding at June 30; a generic warrant blocker would be false. Series G preferred and acquisition consideration still require explicit treatment. Exclusive NRL BDNL licensing is not exclusivity over all BlackDiamond materials: BD2/BD6 have competing suppliers. The relevant scarce capability is qualified materials plus design/manufacturing integration and program position, not merely the word photonics. Sources D01–D03.

**Illustrative inference, not a company forecast or price target:** a 10x per-share outcome with 25% future share growth requires approximately 12.5x today's basic equity value, or $9.5bn. At 25x free cash flow this implies roughly $380m annual FCF; at 20% FCF margin it implies $1.9bn revenue. The assumptions are intentionally explicit. They show why a promising business still needs a credible scale and dilution bridge before challenging an existing allocation.

## Lane changes and semantic hygiene

1. Add an emerging **advanced infrared materials and systems** sublane. LPTH belongs here; use suitable LASR and optical peers without assuming identical products or AI exposure.
2. Add an emerging **industrial process heat and thermal storage** sublane under unknown future bottlenecks. Temperature, duty cycle, electricity economics and acceptance govern competition between nuclear heat and thermal storage. BRNX is a fragile equity expression of a real demand problem.
3. Populate strategic-critical-materials research proxies: existing R2 USAR, R1 ALOY/REA, new IMC and proposed MKAR. An empty proxy list is stale. Do not promote the lane merely because more tickers exist.
4. Preserve identity history: WISESAT_PRELISTING to SAIQ; HVII/HVIIU/HVIIR to ONEN; BNRG to BRNX; BLBX to ALOY. One issuer appearing as stock, unit and right is not three leads.
5. Name-only scan candidates and zero ticker-seeded misses do not establish semantic completeness. Organic expected-proxy misses require separate disposition. ALOY's stale business description demonstrates the practical risk.
