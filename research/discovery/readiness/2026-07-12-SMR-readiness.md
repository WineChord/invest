## Bottleneck Fit

NuScale owns the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission-approved US460 small modular reactor design. The real bottleneck is converting licensed design work into funded, permitted, financeable and constructible plants with credible levelized economics and a supply chain that can deliver. NuScale is a direct U.S.-listed common-stock expression of regulatory and project-development scarcity, but design approval alone does not establish binding project economics or common-shareholder returns.

## Analysis

NuScale is a stronger regulatory comparator than a pre-license reactor story, but almost all current value depends on future projects that still need customer funding, construction budgets, schedules and economics. The single-quarter operating cash-use figure includes a large project contribution and should not be annualized mechanically, yet it demonstrates that commercialization can consume capital long before module revenue. LEU offers an operating fuel bottleneck with a firm government contract, while OKLO and NNE retain different licensing and deployment risks. NuScale improves lane completeness but does not beat LEU or cash at the current entry.
