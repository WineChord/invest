## Bottleneck Fit

FIP owns rail, terminal, port and other infrastructure assets. The June acquisition of Tidewater Logistics added barge and rail transloading in Ohio, West Virginia and Texas for approximately USD 45 million, funded by an upsized term loan; management estimates USD 9 million of next-twelve-month adjusted EBITDA.

## Evidence Gathered

The direct node exposure is real, but the March-quarter filing reports USD 3.84 billion of total debt, USD 154.5 million of net loss attributable to common shareholders, preferred and noncontrolling claims, meaningful customer concentration and external management with annual and incentive fees. Asset rotation and the Long Ridge history make consolidated common-equity cash conversion hard to underwrite. The July 27 completed close was USD 4.155, but a low nominal price does not repair leverage or governance.
