## Bottleneck Fit

Gemini operates a digital-asset exchange, OTC trading, custody, a credit card and the GUSD stablecoin. Revenue comes primarily from transaction fees, with additional custody, withdrawal, card, advisory and stablecoin reserve interest. The issuer owns regulated operating infrastructure, but most current value capture remains dependent on retail crypto prices and trading volume rather than a durable programmable-dollar toll point.

## Analysis

The company has real licenses, customers and operating infrastructure, and the subsequent private placement improves liquidity. Those facts do not establish mission-consistent per-share compounding. Current economics are highly sensitive to crypto prices and retail volume; operating loss and cash use overwhelm revenue; transaction losses, collateral requirements and related-party dealings add tail risk; and GUSD reserve interest is not yet a durable network-economics engine. CRCL is a cleaner public programmable-dollar comparator even though it also remains no-buy.
