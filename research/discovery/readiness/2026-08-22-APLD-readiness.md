The annual filing also consolidates majority-owned ChronoScale and states that reporting or liquidity problems at that subsidiary can affect APLD.

This is not a complete diluted valuation because convertible securities, awards, subsidiary interests and project claims still require scenario treatment.

Same-lane comparison:

- IREN already supplies a 1.86 percent NAV starter with one accepted 50 MW deployment. Its scale blockers are GAAP AI-cloud margin, project cash returns, remaining capex, Mirantis and capitalization evidence.
- APLD supplies larger contracted-campus directness but adds approximately USD 5.307 billion of debt obligations, approximately USD 3.042 billion of planned capex, restricted-cash constraints, customer concentration and disclosed parent-recourse risk.
- RIOT, SEI, WYFI, WULF and CIFR retain their own financing, customer-credit, construction and dilution blockers. None converts APLD's project-risk exposure into a cleaner current entry.
