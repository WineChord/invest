## Conclusion

Cipher Digital is a material AI-compute and power-infrastructure comparator with real hyperscaler contracts, not a community-only crypto pivot. It remains `research_only`: buy 0 at the July 15 completed close of USD 19.86 because delivery, rent, free cash flow, debt service, completion guarantees, bitcoin exposure, customer concentration, and dilution leave inadequate failure tolerance.

## Bottleneck and mission fit

Cipher controls large Texas power and data-center sites and has 600 megawatts of contracted capacity involving Amazon Web Services and Fluidstack with Google support, plus a third long-term investment-grade hyperscaler lease. Scarce power, interconnection, construction speed, and financed capacity are genuine AI-infrastructure bottlenecks. The exposure is more direct than a speculative AI name pivot, but the equity still carries legacy bitcoin economics and project-finance risk.
