## Amendment and Transition

- The monthly full operating cycle is the first Saturday at 15:00 `Asia/Shanghai`. The completed October cycle satisfies October; the next scheduled cycle is November 7.
- The prior weekly authorization ends on October 6. Historical ledger events retain their original dates, amounts, sources, and authorization identity.
- The new monthly authorization is effective October 7 and covers USD 888 on the first calendar day of each month. Its first new occurrence is November 1. Do not create an additional October contribution or any subsequent weekly occurrence.
- Contributions and full decisions are separate. The recurring amount is not a sizing cap or a purchase requirement.
- Deterministic market-day data and validation continue. Material user-triggered events receive timely research and a full decision when allocation is affected. No additional daily or weekly qualitative schedule is created.
- README files contain neither these operating instructions nor contribution amounts. Canonical rules remain in the policy, plan, agent instructions, specification, execution template, command reference, and repository skill.

## Failure Modes Addressed

Monthly dates are generated by calendar month, including year transitions and February, rather than fixed 30-day increments. Authorization validation accepts only the approved monthly first-day schedule or the historical weekly Friday contract. The canonical event ID records its cadence.

A no-due transition preserves the latest validated historical standing-contribution date, preventing an otherwise unchanged October check from rewriting account state. Broker evidence can still correct a superseded same-account contribution; the correction links and pauses the historical authorization and current authorization without relaxing the existing validator.

Catch-up remains capped at eight separate occurrences per run. Duplicate detection, account-state reconciliation, crash recovery, unchanged positions, unchanged broker reconciliation, and the no-trading boundary remain in force.
