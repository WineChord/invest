## Promotion thesis

RIOT now controls and has begun monetizing scarce energized land through a delivered 25 MW AMD lease and a newly signed 191 MW long-duration AI lease. The new agreement, disclosed project capex and initial bridge financing make C+ research-only status too low because the company can now affect the powered-site opportunity-cost ranking. Watch/R2 is sufficient: public evidence does not yet establish the legal tenant support, permanent financing, project IRR or fully diluted per-share value required for active status.
