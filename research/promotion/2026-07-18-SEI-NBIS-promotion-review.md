- Policy version: `v1.1`

- Public-release status: public-safe no-action research
- Required roles completed: evidence/freshness, valuation/entry, bull, bear, opportunity-cost/allocation, and source/process red team

## Decisions

- SEI: promote from `research_only B` to `watch B`; buy 0. Do not promote to `active_candidate` or a buy zone.
- NBIS: retain `watch B+`; buy 0. The new facility does not clear promotion or entry.

## SEI gate review

Mission passes for ongoing monitoring: Solaris directly supplies behind-the-meter generation, control, distribution, commissioning, and operations that can shorten AI data-center time to power. Q1 revenue of USD 196.239 million, about 910 MW deployed, more than 1.1 GW of new ten-year contracts, and USD 3.5025 billion of future minimum lease payments provide enough operating proof for `watch` status.

Evidence, entry, survival, and opportunity-cost gates fail for active promotion or a buy. One customer supplied 52% of Q1 revenue; remaining 2026 capex was about USD 1.263 billion; the company closed USD 1.3 billion of notes and a USD 650 million secured revolver; project debt, variable-interest entities, non-controlling interests, an Up-C structure, equity issuance, and the GESA acquisition complicate common-share economics. Provider-reported market capitalization is about USD 4.32 billion, but Q1 economic units plus GESA shares imply at least roughly USD 4.60 billion at the close before awards and convertible dilution. Current enterprise value is not reconciled.

The reviewer disagreement is resolved by separating monitoring quality from buy eligibility. Freshness review preferred `research_only` because capitalization and cash conversion remain uncertain; bull, valuation, and allocation reviews supported `watch` because real revenue, deployed capacity, contracts, and strategic directness exceed the durable-monitoring threshold. `Watch B` records that evidence without weakening any buy gate.

SEI remains `not_in_buy_zone`. The USD 38.50 alert is only a review trigger, not an intrinsic value, limit price, or standing order; it also requires Q2 cash conversion, deployment progress, pro-forma debt/equity/VIE reconciliation, and no worsening customer concentration.

## NBIS gate review

The USD 775 million secured GPU facility, Microsoft delivery evidence, Q1 revenue growth, and USD 9.2982 billion Q1 cash strengthen the thesis and funding path. They do not clear secured leverage, customer and utilization concentration, USD 2.473 billion Q1 capex, free-cash-flow conversion, or dilution. Correct basic outstanding shares are 253,898,194 after excluding treasury stock, with another 21,065,936 pre-funded warrants. At USD 177.71, basic equity value is roughly USD 45.12 billion, about 85 times trailing revenue; current enterprise value is not yet reconciled.

NBIS remains `watch B+` and `not_in_buy_zone`. The USD 115 alert is only a rerun trigger and also requires complete capitalization, utilization, customer, and cash-conversion evidence.

## Opportunity cost and quantity

Confirmed cash ranks first for current allocation. LEU, GILT, and NBIS are the first conditional re-reviews after their named evidence gates; SEI is the highest-priority newly surfaced research opportunity. Every new buy quantity is zero. Existing holdings remain 17 RKLB and 11 ASTS, with no sale.
