# LEU Promotion and BE Freeze Review — 2026-07-11

Policy: v1.1. Retrieved: 2026-07-11. This review changes research status only; it does not execute or record a trade.

## LEU — promote to active candidate, keep outside the buy zone

The mission gate passes because Centrus is the most direct policy-eligible public expression of scarce U.S. HALEU enrichment capacity. The evidence gate passes for promotion: the Department of Energy award provides up to USD 900 million of firm fixed-price milestone payments for new capability and includes later discretionary delivery options. The entry gate fails today. The full contract is not expected to be filed until the next 10-Q; milestone definitions, cost sharing, convenience termination, appropriations, default remedies, asset ownership in failure states, and complete per-share economics are not yet auditable.

At the 2026-07-10 close of USD 171.05, SEC-derived market capitalization was about USD 2.34 billion, enterprise value about USD 1.61 billion, TTM revenue about USD 379.2 million, and price-to-sales about 6.17. Those headline ratios do not resolve fixed-price cost risk. Q1 revenue was USD 76.7 million, gross profit USD 31.5 million, operating income only USD 0.8 million, operating cash flow negative USD 35.1 million, and reported net income benefited from investment and debt-extinguishment gains. Centrus also faces TENEX/Russia supply exposure, large Oak Ridge and Piketon expansion needs, about USD 1.176 billion debt, and an effective USD 1 billion shelf.

Independent reviewers disagreed on the exact entry: the bull and valuation reviewers considered one share at no more than USD 172 defensible as a roughly 4.2% starter; the bear reviewer vetoed even that until full contract disclosure. The main-agent reconciliation chooses no trade. Cash has a higher current risk-adjusted value because RKLB and ASTS already occupy 53.83% of the account and the missing contract terms are issuer-controlled public disclosure, not unfinished repository research.

## BE — freeze and prohibit new capital

Bloom's 2026-07-09 8-K categorically denies the short report's accounting and scandium claims and states it has sufficient supply without depending on China. It does not disclose supplier identities, contracted quantities, inventory, scandium intensity, a terminal-customer bridge, or a reconciliation among revenue, backlog, and remaining performance obligations. Bloom's Q1 10-Q independently shows two counterparties represented 50% and 12% of revenue and explains that a contractual customer can be a project-finance affiliate rather than the terminal user. The short seller is economically conflicted, so its claims are treated as allegations, but the company response is not sufficient quantitative rebuttal.

The evidence gate therefore fails for new capital. The entry gate also fails independently because the 2026-07-10 close of USD 244.61 implied about USD 69.6 billion market capitalization and about 35 times TTM sales. BE moves from watch to frozen until it publishes an auditable supply and accounting reconciliation.
