## Decision

Raise IREN research priority from `B` to `B+`, retain `watch`, and keep buy quantity at zero. The July 20 contract update materially strengthens the demand and financing thesis, but it does not clear the evidence, entry, survival-adjusted valuation, or promotion gates.

## Role synthesis

- Evidence and freshness: USD 2.8 billion of new multi-year contracts, approximately four-year weighted-average terms, customer prepayments covering about 45% of associated GPU capex, and approximately USD 7.6 billion preliminary cash including USD 1.7 billion restricted cash are primary-source positive facts. Contract-level pricing, termination, credit support, SLAs, customer concentration, margins, remaining capex, current debt, and accepted revenue are unavailable.
- Bull case: 480 MW of targeted 2026 AI-cloud capacity, approximately 85% contracted targeted ARR, prepayments, and customer diversification could make IREN a direct power-and-compute scarcity converter at a scale not reflected by historical mining comparisons.
- Bear case: issuer-defined ARR assumes delivery, commissioning, testing, acceptance, pricing, and utilization. It is not GAAP revenue or free cash flow. Hardware obsolescence, construction, project finance, customer concentration, and residual bitcoin exposure can transfer economics away from common shareholders.
- Valuation and entry: the USD 40.20 July 20 close rose 19.57% in one session while remaining approximately 30.82% below the 21-session comparison close. That volatility is a research trigger, not a buy zone. Current enterprise value and committed capital cannot be reconciled reliably enough for sizing.
- Opportunity cost and allocation: IREN becomes the highest-priority non-space research follow-up, ahead of GILT, LEU, CRDO, and MDA for immediate work, but confirmed cash remains superior until the missing contract and capitalization evidence exists.
- Source red team: the filing supports contracted capacity and targets, not recognized revenue, margin, free cash flow, or guaranteed customer performance. No model may capitalize the target ARR as current revenue.

## Gate assessment

- Mission gate: conditional pass. A scaled power-backed AI-cloud platform could produce asymmetric upside without adding to the account's space concentration.
- Evidence gate: fail for active promotion. Demand is better evidenced, but economics and delivery remain incomplete.
- Entry gate: fail. Post-announcement repricing and unreconciled financing leave insufficient downside tolerance.
- Survival gate: conditional. Cash and prepayments help, while restricted cash, remaining capex, debt, commissioning, and customer obligations remain material.
- Opportunity-cost gate: fail for capital today; pass for higher research priority.

Reopen after filed contract economics, customer concentration, margin and capex disclosure, accepted revenue-producing GPU deployments, reconciled unrestricted liquidity and debt, or a thesis-preserving valuation reset.
