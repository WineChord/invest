The offering was expected to close after this review, final capitalization remains incomplete, and the account already has 53.83% exposure to space-related holdings. MDA therefore displaces future RKLB or ASTS additions in the comparison set, but it does not receive a public buy zone now.

Gilat owns satellite modems, terminals, amplifiers, software, field-service capability, and customer relationships. Q1 revenue grew 20% and adjusted EBITDA nearly doubled. The proposed USD 157.5 million Comtech satellite-and-space acquisition is expected to create more than USD 700 million in annual revenue and about USD 80 million in adjusted EBITDA, while the July 7 USD 11 million U.S. defense orders provide another direct demand signal.

The mission, evidence, and survival gates pass for active-candidate status. The entry case is attractive relative to public space peers, but the acquisition forecasts are management-adjusted, the deal still needs regulatory approval and integration, acquisition funding consumes much of the prior net-cash cushion, and the account's existing space concentration makes even a cheap same-lane starter less useful than a non-space bottleneck opportunity. The bull reviewer supported a tiny live entry; the bear and allocation review rejected increasing space exposure. The main reconciliation promotes GILT while keeping the public buy-zone status trigger-only until Comtech closing, combined-margin evidence, cash conversion, or a sufficiently large thesis-preserving dislocation.
