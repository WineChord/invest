# Policy Records

Current policy: [policy-v1.2.md](policy-v1.2.md)

Historical policies remain in this directory for auditability. New monthly decisions should cite the current policy unless they are intentionally analyzing a historical decision under the policy that was active at that time.

Policy `v1.2` makes Article 1 controlling at the operating level. It keeps the approved liquidity-reserve rule, adds mission-accountability reviews for prolonged high liquidity-option weight, requires portfolio-impact and path-to-scale analysis, distinguishes target readiness from repository health, and gives repeated no-action decisions an explicit burden of proof.

Public release is governed by [../../PUBLICATION_POLICY.md](../../PUBLICATION_POLICY.md). Policy `v1.2` allows transparency only after delayed publication, redaction, no personalized public advice, and no compensated promotional activity.
