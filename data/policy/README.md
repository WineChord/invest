# Policy Records

Current policy: [policy-v1.3.md](policy-v1.3.md)

Historical policies remain in this directory for auditability. New allocation decisions should cite the current policy unless they are intentionally analyzing a historical decision under the policy that was active at that time.

Policy `v1.3` keeps Article 1 controlling and preserves the `v1.2` mission-accountability, liquidity-reserve, portfolio-impact, and no-action review controls. It changes the default funding cadence to USD 888 every Friday and adds a narrow, revocable, idempotent account-owner standing authorization for that deposit only.

Public release is governed by [../../PUBLICATION_POLICY.md](../../PUBLICATION_POLICY.md). Policy `v1.3` allows transparency only after delayed publication, redaction, no personalized public advice, and no compensated promotional activity.
