# Policy Records

Current policy: [policy-v1.1.md](policy-v1.1.md)

Historical policies remain in this directory for auditability. New monthly decisions should cite the current policy unless they are intentionally analyzing a historical decision under the policy that was active at that time.

Policy `v1.1` adds the approved liquidity-reserve rule: SGOV or a materially equivalent short-duration U.S. Treasury ETF or Treasury money-market vehicle may be used for cash management only. It does not change the account's return-seeking mission.

Public release is governed by [../../PUBLICATION_POLICY.md](../../PUBLICATION_POLICY.md). Policy `v1.1` allows transparency only after delayed publication, redaction, no personalized public advice, and no compensated promotional activity.
