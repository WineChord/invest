# Policy v1.3 Proposal

Proposal ID: `policy-v1.3-weekly-standing-contribution`

Status: approved

Approved by: account owner

Approved at: 2026-07-29

Proposed policy version: `v1.3`

Supersedes: `v1.2`

## Article 1 Preflight

The account needs a durable funding path large enough to build and scale rare qualifying positions when evidence and entry conditions align. Moving the default contribution from USD 888 per month to USD 888 per week increases the potential annual funding path from approximately USD 10,656 to USD 46,176 without creating a mandatory trading cadence.

The change serves Article 1 only if funding truth remains narrow, deterministic, reversible, and separate from investment qualification. It must not turn a calendar event into evidence for a trade or weaken the mission, evidence, entry, survival, and opportunity-cost gates.

## Problem

The prior policy treated each planned contribution as unconfirmed until a separate occurrence-level confirmation arrived. That preserved truth but added repetitive friction after the account owner established a stable recurring funding instruction.

The repository already separates deposits from trades through an append-only ledger. It can therefore support a narrow standing authorization for one fixed deposit while preserving stricter confirmation requirements for every other account fact.

## Approved Change

Adopt an active, revocable standing account-owner authorization with these exact terms:

- USD 888;
- every Friday in `Asia/Shanghai`;
- first new occurrence on 2026-07-31;
- no backfill before the effective date;
- each due date recorded as one separate ledger event;
- no occurrence-level reconfirmation;
- missed runs caught up as separate dated events within the canonical fail-closed bound;
- no automatic trade, reserve purchase, position change, fee, cost-basis change, or tax-lot change.

The authorization is an account-owner attestation that the scheduled contribution is deposited and available for trading on each due date. It is not a live broker API observation and must not advance the broker-reconciliation timestamp.

## Truth and Conflict Handling

Broker evidence remains superior. If later broker evidence shows that an occurrence did not arrive or was unavailable:

1. keep the original ledger row;
2. append a machine-linked correction rather than editing history;
3. pause the standing authorization with status `paused_broker_conflict`;
4. do not resume future occurrences until the conflict is resolved under the account-confirmation workflow.

A recurring occurrence is satisfied only by its canonical authorization identity and complete economics. A truly separate same-account, same-date, same-currency, same-amount deposit must use the explicit `user_confirmed_additional_deposit` source; otherwise automatic application stops on ambiguity.

## Deterministic Application

Before qualitative routing, the scheduled process must:

1. load the canonical account plan;
2. enumerate due Fridays from the effective date through the local as-of date;
3. deduplicate by broker, account alias, authorization identity, due date, amount, currency, and full event economics, while blocking ambiguous same-day collisions;
4. append only missing occurrences;
5. preserve the due date as trade and settlement date and the actual write time as `created_at`;
6. reconcile confirmed cash from the append-only ledger and add the authorized amount to settled cash and buying power;
7. leave positions and the broker-reconciliation timestamp unchanged;
8. fail closed on ambiguous duplicates, account-state drift, an inactive plan, or a broker conflict.

The Friday contribution does not force an investment decision or a report. The normal Article 1 opportunity sentinel and Saturday operating cycle decide whether the confirmed liquidity should be deployed.

## Article 1 Postflight

The change increases the account's capacity to fund mission-relevant positions without lowering the threshold for buying them. It preserves human control over trades, append-only truth, broker-evidence priority, auditability, and correction semantics. Weekly funding is a means to Article 1, not a requirement to trade weekly or to deploy every contribution.
