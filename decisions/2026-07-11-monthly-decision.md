## Why the Held Positions Are Not Sold

RKLB and ASTS are high-risk, high-duration companies, but neither has produced primary evidence of a thesis break. Selling solely to make weights look tidy would turn valuation discomfort into a realized decision without a broken mission, evidence, or survival gate. The correct response is to stop adding and define explicit rerun triggers.

RKLB must be rerun if Iridium consideration or financing worsens, regulatory approval is materially obstructed, Neutron suffers a major delay without a credible recovery plan, low-price equity financing accelerates, or launch/space-systems margin and backlog conversion deteriorate. ASTS must be rerun if BlueBird on-orbit performance fails, commercial service lacks verified coverage/economics, spectrum or regulatory approvals fail, financing becomes punitive, or Starlink Mobile establishes a durable cost and coverage advantage ASTS cannot answer.

## LEU Promotion Without a Buy

Centrus passes the mission gate and the promotion evidence gate. The USD 900 million Department of Energy contract confirms that U.S. HALEU enrichment is a strategic bottleneck. At USD 171.05, SEC-derived market capitalization is about USD 2.34 billion, enterprise value about USD 1.61 billion, and corrected price-to-sales about 5.17. Those numbers are less extreme than most watchlist growth names. The post-cycle audit also confirmed that ACO would obtain title to the deployed enrichment capacity after contract completion, strengthening value capture and changing the private action assessment.

The entry gate still fails. The award is firm fixed price, is paid against milestones, targets only one MTU of HALEU by March 2032 in the base scope, and leaves two later five-MTU options entirely discretionary. A 10% cost overrun on USD 900 million is about USD 90 million; an 8% contract margin is only USD 72 million. The next 10-Q is expected to contain the complete contract, including terms that can determine whether the award creates per-share value or merely funds a national capability at weak returns. Q1 operating income was only USD 0.8 million, operating cash flow was negative USD 35.1 million, TENEX/Russia exposure remains material, expansion needs are capital-intensive, debt is about USD 1.176 billion, and a USD 1 billion shelf is available. Promotion improves monitoring priority; it does not compel an order.
