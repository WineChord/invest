## Decision conclusion

MDA remains the strongest current scale-up candidate, but its August 7 close of USD 34.59 is effectively at the USD 34.60 research ceiling while Q2 free cash flow, post-offering dilution and the pro-forma economics of Blue Canyon and CLS remain unresolved. The ceiling is a research boundary, not an order or an executable limit.

A hypothetical three-share MDA add would raise MDA to about 3.20% and space exposure to about 25.77%; that loss budget is tolerable, so concentration alone is not the veto. The better reason to wait is that one event day has limited expected waiting cost and unusually high information value.
