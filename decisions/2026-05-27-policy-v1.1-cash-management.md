## Schwab International Operating Note

The user stated the account is a Schwab International account opened from mainland China.

Based on Schwab International and Investor.gov materials retrieved on 2026-05-27:

- Schwab One International accounts can trade U.S. stocks and ETFs, subject to eligibility and account-specific restrictions.
- SGOV is an ETF and therefore a security, not cash.
- U.S. stocks and ETFs generally settle T+1.
- In a Schwab cash account, unsettled proceeds may be available to trade, but selling the newly purchased security before the original sale settles can create a good faith violation.

Operationally, if the account holds SGOV and wants to buy ASTS:

1. Sell SGOV during a trading session.
2. Wait for the SGOV sell order to execute.
3. Confirm Schwab shows sufficient `Funds Available To Trade`.
4. Buy ASTS or another approved target.
5. Do not sell the newly purchased stock before the SGOV sale settles unless Schwab confirms there is no settlement violation.

## Decision Discipline Going Forward

Does any candidate currently pass the mission gate, evidence gate, and entry gate strongly enough to deserve capital now?

If not, the correct answer can be no trade, hold cash, or use SGOV as a liquidity reserve after broker-confirmed execution.

If yes, the correct answer can be to sell some or all SGOV and deploy more than the latest monthly contribution, provided the decision explains why the opportunity deserves reserve capital, what settlement constraints apply, and what risk buffer remains.
